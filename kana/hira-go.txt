title: Hiragana GO
kana: ご
script: hiragana
romaji: go
key: go
base: こ
sound: like the "go" in go
mnemonic_word: ko to go
mnemonic: こ plus two little marks makes ご. The marks make the sound buzz: ko turns into go!

[words]
ごはん | gohan | rice, meal
ごご | gogo | afternoon
ごみ | gomi | trash

[similar]
こ | ko | ご has two small marks at the top right. こ has none.

[distractors]
ko, ga
