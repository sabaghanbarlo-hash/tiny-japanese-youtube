title: Hiragana ZU
kana: ず
script: hiragana
romaji: zu
key: zu
base: す
sound: like the "zoo"
mnemonic_word: su to zu
mnemonic: す plus two little marks makes ず. The marks make the sound buzz: su turns into zu!

[words]
ずぼん | zubon | trousers
ちず | chizu | map
ずっと | zutto | all along

[similar]
す | su | ず has two small marks at the top right. す has none.
づ | zu | ず and づ sound the same. ず is used far more often.

[distractors]
su, ze
