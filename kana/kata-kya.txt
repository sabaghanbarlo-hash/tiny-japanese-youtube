title: Katakana KYA
kana: キャ
script: katakana
romaji: kya
sound: one quick sound, "ki" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: ki + ya
mnemonic: キ plus a small ャ makes キャ. Say ki and ya together as one quick sound: kya!

[words]
キャベツ | kyabetsu | cabbage
キャンプ | kyanpu | camp
キャッシュ | kyasshu | cash

[similar]
キ | ki | キャ has a small ャ at the bottom right. キ alone is just ki.
