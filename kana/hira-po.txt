title: Hiragana PO
kana: ぽ
script: hiragana
romaji: po
key: po
base: ほ
sound: like the "po" in pole
mnemonic_word: ho to po
mnemonic: ほ plus a little circle makes ぽ. The circle turns the h sound into a puff of air: ho becomes po!

[words]
さんぽ | sanpo | walk
ぽけっと | poketto | pocket
ぽすと | posuto | mailbox

[similar]
ほ | ho | ぽ has a small circle at the top right. ほ has none.

[distractors]
ho, pa
