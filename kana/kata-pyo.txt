title: Katakana PYO
kana: ピョ
script: katakana
romaji: pyo
sound: one quick sound, "pi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: pi + yo
mnemonic: ピ plus a small ョ makes ピョ. Say pi and yo together as one quick sound: pyo!

[words]
ピョン | pyon | hop
ピョンピョン | pyonpyon | hopping

[similar]
ピ | pi | ピョ has a small ョ at the bottom right. ピ alone is just pi.

[distractors]
pya, pyu
