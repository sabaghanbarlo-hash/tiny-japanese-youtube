title: Katakana JU
kana: ジュ
script: katakana
romaji: ju
sound: one quick sound, "ji" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: ji + yu
mnemonic: ジ plus a small ュ makes ジュ. Say ji and yu together as one quick sound: ju!

[words]
ジュース | juusu | juice
ジュエリー | juerii | jewelry
ジュニア | junia | junior

[similar]
ジ | ji | ジュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. ジ alone is just ji.

[distractors]
ja, jo
