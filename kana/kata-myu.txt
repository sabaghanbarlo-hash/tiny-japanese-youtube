title: Katakana MYU
kana: ミュ
script: katakana
romaji: myu
sound: one quick sound, "mi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: mi + yu
mnemonic: ミ plus a small ュ makes ミュ. Say mi and yu together as one quick sound: myu!

[words]
ミュージック | myuujikku | music
ミュージカル | myuujikaru | musical
ミュール | myuuru | mule

[similar]
ミ | mi | ミュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. ミ alone is just mi.

[distractors]
mya, myo
