title: Hiragana GYO
kana: ぎょ
script: hiragana
romaji: gyo
sound: one quick sound, "gi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: gi + yo
mnemonic: ぎ plus a small ょ makes ぎょ. Say gi and yo together as one quick sound: gyo!

[words]
ぎょうざ | gyouza | dumplings
ぎょうれつ | gyouretsu | line
きんぎょ | kingyo | goldfish

[similar]
ぎ | gi | ぎょ has a small ょ at the bottom right. ぎ alone is just gi.

[distractors]
gya, gyu
