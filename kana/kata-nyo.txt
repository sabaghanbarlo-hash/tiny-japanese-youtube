title: Katakana NYO
kana: ニョ
script: katakana
romaji: nyo
sound: one quick sound, "ni" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: ni + yo
mnemonic: ニ plus a small ョ makes ニョ. Say ni and yo together as one quick sound: nyo!

[words]
ニョッキ | nyokki | gnocchi
ニョロニョロ | nyoronyoro | wriggling

[similar]
ニ | ni | ニョ has a small ョ at the bottom right. ニ alone is just ni.

[distractors]
nya, nyu
