title: Hiragana ZU (づ)
kana: づ
script: hiragana
romaji: zu
key: dzu
base: つ
sound: exactly like ず (zu)
mnemonic_word: tsu to zu
mnemonic: つ plus two little marks makes づ. The marks make the sound buzz: tsu turns into zu!

[words]
つづく | tsuzuku | to continue
みかづき | mikazuki | crescent moon
こづつみ | kozutsumi | parcel

[similar]
つ | tsu | づ has two small marks at the top right. つ has none.
ず | zu | づ sounds exactly like ず. ず is used far more often.

[distractors]
da, ji
