title: Katakana JI (ヂ)
kana: ヂ
script: katakana
romaji: ji
key: dji
base: チ
sound: exactly like ジ (ji)
mnemonic_word: chi to ji
mnemonic: チ plus two little marks makes ヂ. The marks make the sound buzz: chi turns into ji!

[words]
ヂ | ji | very rare spelling, same sound as ジ

[similar]
チ | chi | ヂ has two small marks at the top right. チ has none.
ジ | ji | ヂ sounds exactly like ジ. ジ is used far more often.

[distractors]
da, zu
