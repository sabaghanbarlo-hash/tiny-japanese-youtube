title: Hiragana MYU
kana: みゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: myu
sound: one quick sound, "mi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: mi + yu
mnemonic: み plus a small ゅ makes みゅ. Say mi and yu together as one quick sound: myu!

[words]
みゅう | myuu | mew sound
みゅうみゅう | myuumyuu | mewing

[similar]
み | mi | みゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. み alone is just mi.

[distractors]
mya, myo
