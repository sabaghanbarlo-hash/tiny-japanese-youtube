title: Katakana PO
kana: ポ
script: katakana
romaji: po
key: po
base: ホ
sound: like the "po" in pole
mnemonic_word: ho to po
mnemonic: ホ plus a little circle makes ポ. The circle turns the h sound into a puff of air: ho becomes po!

[words]
ポスト | posuto | mailbox
ポケット | poketto | pocket
ポテト | poteto | potato

[similar]
ホ | ho | ポ has a small circle at the top right. ホ has none.

[distractors]
ho, pa
