title: Hiragana CHA
kana: ちゃ
script: hiragana
romaji: cha
sound: one quick sound, "chi" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: chi + ya
mnemonic: ち plus a small ゃ makes ちゃ. Say chi and ya together as one quick sound: cha!

[words]
おちゃ | ocha | tea
ちゃわん | chawan | rice bowl
ちゃいろ | chairo | brown

[similar]
ち | chi | ちゃ has a small ゃ at the bottom right. ち alone is just chi.

[distractors]
chu, cho
