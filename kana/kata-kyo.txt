title: Katakana KYO
kana: キョ
script: katakana
romaji: kyo
sound: one quick sound, "ki" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: ki + yo
mnemonic: キ plus a small ョ makes キョ. Say ki and yo together as one quick sound: kyo!

[words]
キョロキョロ | kyorokyoro | looking around
キョン | kyon | muntjac deer

[similar]
キ | ki | キョ has a small ョ at the bottom right. キ alone is just ki.

[distractors]
kya, kyu
