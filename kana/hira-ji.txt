title: Hiragana JI
kana: じ
script: hiragana
romaji: ji
key: ji
base: し
sound: like the "jee" in jeep
mnemonic_word: shi to ji
mnemonic: し plus two little marks makes じ. The marks make the sound buzz: shi turns into ji!

[words]
じしょ | jisho | dictionary
じかん | jikan | time
じてんしゃ | jitensha | bicycle

[similar]
し | shi | じ has two small marks at the top right. し has none.
ぢ | ji | じ and ぢ sound the same. じ is used far more often.

[distractors]
shi, zu
