title: Katakana CHO
kana: チョ
script: katakana
romaji: cho
sound: one quick sound, "chi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: chi + yo
mnemonic: チ plus a small ョ makes チョ. Say chi and yo together as one quick sound: cho!

チョーク | chooku | chalk
チョコ | choko | choco

[similar]
