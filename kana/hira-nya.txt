title: Hiragana NYA
kana: にゃ
script: hiragana
romaji: nya
sound: one quick sound, "ni" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: ni + ya
mnemonic: に plus a small ゃ makes にゃ. Say ni and ya together as one quick sound: nya!

[words]
こんにゃく | konnyaku | konjac
にゃあ | nyaa | meow
にゃんこ | nyanko | kitty

[similar]
に | ni | にゃ has a small ゃ at the bottom right. に alone is just ni.

[distractors]
nyu, nyo
