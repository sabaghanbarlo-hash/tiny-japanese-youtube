title: Hiragana HYO
kana: ひょ
script: hiragana
romaji: hyo
sound: one quick sound, "hi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: hi + yo
mnemonic: ひ plus a small ょ makes ひょ. Say hi and yo together as one quick sound: hyo!

[words]
ひょう | hyou | leopard
ひょうげん | hyougen | expression
ひょうしき | hyoushiki | sign

[similar]
ひ | hi | ひょ has a small ょ at the bottom right. ひ alone is just hi.

[distractors]
hya, hyu
