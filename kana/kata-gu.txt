title: Katakana GU
kana: グ
script: katakana
romaji: gu
key: gu
base: ク
sound: like the "goo" in goose
mnemonic_word: ku to gu
mnemonic: ク plus two little marks makes グ. The marks make the sound buzz: ku turns into gu!

[words]
グラス | gurasu | drinking glass
グループ | guruupu | group
グラム | guramu | gram

[similar]
ク | ku | グ has two small marks at the top right. ク has none.

[distractors]
ku, ge
