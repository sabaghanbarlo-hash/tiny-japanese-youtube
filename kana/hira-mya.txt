title: Hiragana MYA
kana: みゃ
script: hiragana
romaji: mya
sound: one quick sound, "mi" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: mi + ya
mnemonic: み plus a small ゃ makes みゃ. Say mi and ya together as one quick sound: mya!

[words]
みゃく | myaku | pulse
さんみゃく | sanmyaku | mountain range

[similar]
み | mi | みゃ has a small ゃ at the bottom right. み alone is just mi.

[distractors]
myu, myo
