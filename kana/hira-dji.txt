title: Hiragana JI (ぢ)
kana: ぢ
script: hiragana
romaji: ji
key: dji
base: ち
sound: exactly like じ (ji)
mnemonic_word: chi to ji
mnemonic: ち plus two little marks makes ぢ. The marks make the sound buzz: chi turns into ji!

[words]
はなぢ | hanaji | nosebleed
ちぢむ | chijimu | to shrink

[similar]
ち | chi | ぢ has two small marks at the top right. ち has none.
じ | ji | ぢ sounds exactly like じ. じ is used far more often.

[distractors]
da, zu
