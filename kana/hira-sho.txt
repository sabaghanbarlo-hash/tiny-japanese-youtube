title: Hiragana SHO
kana: しょ
script: hiragana
romaji: sho
sound: one quick sound, "shi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: shi + yo
mnemonic: し plus a small ょ makes しょ. Say shi and yo together as one quick sound: sho!

[words]
しょくじ | shokuji | meal
しょうがつ | shougatsu | New Year
しょるい | shorui | documents

[similar]
し | shi | しょ has a small ょ at the bottom right. し alone is just shi.

[distractors]
sha, shu
