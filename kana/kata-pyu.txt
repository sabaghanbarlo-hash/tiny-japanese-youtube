title: Katakana PYU
kana: ピュ
script: katakana
romaji: pyu
sound: one quick sound, "pi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: pi + yu
mnemonic: ピ plus a small ュ makes ピュ. Say pi and yu together as one quick sound: pyu!

[words]
ピューマ | pyuuma | puma
コンピュータ | konpyuuta | computer
ピューレ | pyuure | puree

[similar]
ピ | pi | ピュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. ピ alone is just pi.

[distractors]
pya, pyo
