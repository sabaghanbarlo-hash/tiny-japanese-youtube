title: Hiragana BA
kana: ば
script: hiragana
romaji: ba
key: ba
base: は
sound: like the "ba" in bar
mnemonic_word: ha to ba
mnemonic: は plus two little marks makes ば. The marks make the sound buzz: ha turns into ba!

ばしょ | basho | place
ばん | ban | evening

[similar]
