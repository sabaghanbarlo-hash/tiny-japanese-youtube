title: Katakana CHU
kana: チュ
script: katakana
romaji: chu
sound: one quick sound, "chi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: chi + yu
mnemonic: チ plus a small ュ makes チュ. Say chi and yu together as one quick sound: chu!

[words]
チューリップ | chuurippu | tulip
チューブ | chuubu | tube
チュニック | chunikku | tunic

[similar]
チ | chi | チュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. チ alone is just chi.

[distractors]
cha, cho
