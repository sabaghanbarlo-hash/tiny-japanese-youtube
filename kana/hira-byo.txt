title: Hiragana BYO
kana: びょ
script: hiragana
romaji: byo
sound: one quick sound, "bi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: bi + yo
mnemonic: び plus a small ょ makes びょ. Say bi and yo together as one quick sound: byo!

[words]
びょうき | byouki | illness
びょういん | byouin | hospital
びょうどう | byoudou | equality

[similar]
び | bi | びょ has a small ょ at the bottom right. び alone is just bi.

[distractors]
bya, byu
