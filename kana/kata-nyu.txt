title: Katakana NYU
kana: ニュ
script: katakana
romaji: nyu
sound: one quick sound, "ni" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: ni + yu
mnemonic: ニ plus a small ュ makes ニュ. Say ni and yu together as one quick sound: nyu!

[words]
ニュース | nyuusu | news
メニュー | menyuu | menu
ニューヨーク | nyuuyooku | New York

[similar]
ニ | ni | ニュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. ニ alone is just ni.

[distractors]
nya, nyo
