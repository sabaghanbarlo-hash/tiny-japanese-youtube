title: Katakana DO
kana: ド
script: katakana
romaji: do
key: do
base: ト
sound: like the "do" in dough
mnemonic_word: to to do
mnemonic: ト plus two little marks makes ド. The marks make the sound buzz: to turns into do!

[words]
ドア | doa | door
ドライブ | doraibu | drive
ドレス | doresu | dress

[similar]
ト | to | ド has two small marks at the top right. ト has none.
