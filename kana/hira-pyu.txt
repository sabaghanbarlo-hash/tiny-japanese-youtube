title: Hiragana PYU
kana: ぴゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: pyu
sound: one quick sound, "pi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: pi + yu
mnemonic: ぴ plus a small ゅ makes ぴゅ. Say pi and yu together as one quick sound: pyu!

[words]
ぴゅうぴゅう | pyuupyuu | whistling
ぴゅっと | pyutto | whoosh

[similar]
ぴ | pi | ぴゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. ぴ alone is just pi.

[distractors]
pya, pyo
