title: Hiragana NYU
kana: にゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: nyu
sound: one quick sound, "ni" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: ni + yu
mnemonic: に plus a small ゅ makes にゅ. Say ni and yu together as one quick sound: nyu!

[words]
ぎゅうにゅう | gyuunyuu | milk
にゅうがく | nyuugaku | school entrance
にゅういん | nyuuin | hospital stay

[similar]
に | ni | にゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. に alone is just ni.

[distractors]
nya, nyo
