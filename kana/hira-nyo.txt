title: Hiragana NYO
kana: にょ
script: hiragana
romaji: nyo
sound: one quick sound, "ni" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: ni + yo
mnemonic: に plus a small ょ makes にょ. Say ni and yo together as one quick sound: nyo!

[words]
にょろにょろ | nyoronyoro | wriggling
にょうぼう | nyoubou | wife

[similar]
に | ni | にょ has a small ょ at the bottom right. に alone is just ni.

[distractors]
nya, nyu
