title: Katakana BYO
kana: ビョ
script: katakana
romaji: byo
sound: one quick sound, "bi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: bi + yo
mnemonic: ビ plus a small ョ makes ビョ. Say bi and yo together as one quick sound: byo!

[words]
ビョーキ | byooki | illness (casual)
ビョウイン | byouin | hospital

[similar]
ビ | bi | ビョ has a small ョ at the bottom right. ビ alone is just bi.

[distractors]
bya, byu
