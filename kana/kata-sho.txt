title: Katakana SHO
kana: ショ
script: katakana
romaji: sho
sound: one quick sound, "shi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: shi + yo
mnemonic: シ plus a small ョ makes ショ. Say shi and yo together as one quick sound: sho!

[words]
ショップ | shoppu | shop
ショー | shoo | show
ショッピング | shoppingu | shopping

[similar]
シ | shi | ショ has a small ョ at the bottom right. シ alone is just shi.

[distractors]
sha, shu
