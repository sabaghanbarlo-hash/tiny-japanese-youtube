title: Katakana ZU
kana: ズ
script: katakana
romaji: zu
key: zu
base: ス
sound: like the "zoo"
mnemonic_word: su to zu
mnemonic: ス plus two little marks makes ズ. The marks make the sound buzz: su turns into zu!

[words]
ズボン | zubon | trousers
ズーム | zuumu | zoom
パズル | pazuru | puzzle

[similar]
ス | su | ズ has two small marks at the top right. ス has none.
ヅ | zu | ズ and ヅ sound the same. ズ is used far more often.

[distractors]
su, ze
