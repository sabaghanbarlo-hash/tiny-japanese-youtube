title: Hiragana BO
kana: ぼ
script: hiragana
romaji: bo
key: bo
base: ほ
sound: like the "bo" in boat
mnemonic_word: ho to bo
mnemonic: ほ plus two little marks makes ぼ. The marks make the sound buzz: ho turns into bo!
