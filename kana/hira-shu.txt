title: Hiragana SHU
kana: しゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: shu
sound: one quick sound, "shi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: shi + yu
mnemonic: し plus a small ゅ makes しゅ. Say shi and yu together as one quick sound: shu!

[words]
しゅくだい | shukudai | homework
しゅみ | shumi | hobby
しゅうまつ | shuumatsu | weekend

[similar]
し | shi | しゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. し alone is just shi.

[distractors]
sha, sho
