title: Katakana BA
kana: バ
script: katakana
romaji: ba
key: ba
base: ハ
sound: like the "ba" in bar
mnemonic_word: ha to ba
mnemonic: ハ plus two little marks makes バ. The marks make the sound buzz: ha turns into ba!

[words]
バス | basu | bus
バナナ | banana | banana
バイク | baiku | motorbike

[similar]
ハ | ha | バ has two small marks at the top right. ハ has none.
パ | pa | バ has two small marks. パ has a small circle.

[distractors]
ha, bi
