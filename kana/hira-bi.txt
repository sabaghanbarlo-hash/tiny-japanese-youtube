title: Hiragana BI
kana: び
script: hiragana
romaji: bi
key: bi
base: ひ
sound: like the "bee"
mnemonic_word: hi to bi
mnemonic: ひ plus two little marks makes び. The marks make the sound buzz: hi turns into bi!

[words]
くび | kubi | neck
えび | ebi | shrimp
びじん | bijin | beautiful woman

[similar]
ひ | hi | び has two small marks at the top right. ひ has none.

[distractors]
hi, bu
