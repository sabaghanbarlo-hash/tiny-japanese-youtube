title: Hiragana CHU
kana: ちゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: chu
sound: one quick sound, "chi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: chi + yu
mnemonic: ち plus a small ゅ makes ちゅ. Say chi and yu together as one quick sound: chu!

[words]
ちゅうごく | chuugoku | China
ちゅうしゃ | chuusha | parking
ちゅうい | chuui | caution

[similar]
ち | chi | ちゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. ち alone is just chi.

[distractors]
cha, cho
