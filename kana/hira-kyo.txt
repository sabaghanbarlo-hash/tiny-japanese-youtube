title: Hiragana KYO
kana: きょ
script: hiragana
romaji: kyo
sound: one quick sound, "ki" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: ki + yo
mnemonic: き plus a small ょ makes きょ. Say ki and yo together as one quick sound: kyo!

[words]
きょう | kyou | today
きょうしつ | kyoushitsu | classroom
きょねん | kyonen | last year

[similar]
き | ki | きょ has a small ょ at the bottom right. き alone is just ki.

[distractors]
kya, kyu
