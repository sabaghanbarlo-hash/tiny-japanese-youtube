title: Hiragana KYU
kana: きゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: kyu
sound: one quick sound, "ki" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: ki + yu
mnemonic: き plus a small ゅ makes きゅ. Say ki and yu together as one quick sound: kyu!

[words]
きゅうり | kyuuri | cucumber
きゅうけい | kyuukei | break
きゅう | kyuu | nine

[similar]
き | ki | きゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. き alone is just ki.

[distractors]
kya, kyo
