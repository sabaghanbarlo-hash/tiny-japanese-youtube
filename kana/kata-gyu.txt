title: Katakana GYU
kana: ギュ
script: katakana
romaji: gyu
sound: one quick sound, "gi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: gi + yu
mnemonic: ギ plus a small ュ makes ギュ. Say gi and yu together as one quick sound: gyu!

[words]
ギュッと | gyutto | tightly
ギュウドン | gyuudon | beef bowl

[similar]
ギ | gi | ギュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. ギ alone is just gi.

[distractors]
gya, gyo
