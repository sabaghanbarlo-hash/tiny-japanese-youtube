title: Katakana PA
kana: パ
script: katakana
romaji: pa
key: pa
base: ハ
sound: like the "pa" in papa
mnemonic_word: ha to pa
mnemonic: ハ plus a little circle makes パ. The circle turns the h sound into a puff of air: ha becomes pa!

[words]
パン | pan | bread
パーティー | paatii | party
パソコン | pasokon | computer

[similar]
ハ | ha | パ has a small circle at the top right. ハ has none.
バ | ba | パ has a small circle. バ has two small marks.

[distractors]
ha, pi
