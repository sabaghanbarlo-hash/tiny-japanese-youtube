title: Katakana HYO
kana: ヒョ
script: katakana
romaji: hyo
sound: one quick sound, "hi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: hi + yo
mnemonic: ヒ plus a small ョ makes ヒョ. Say hi and yo together as one quick sound: hyo!

[words]
ヒョウ | hyou | leopard
ヒョロヒョロ | hyorohyoro | spindly

[similar]
ヒ | hi | ヒョ has a small ョ at the bottom right. ヒ alone is just hi.

[distractors]
hya, hyu
