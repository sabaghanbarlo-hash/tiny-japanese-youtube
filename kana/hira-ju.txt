title: Hiragana JU
kana: じゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: ju
sound: one quick sound, "ji" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: ji + yu
mnemonic: じ plus a small ゅ makes じゅ. Say ji and yu together as one quick sound: ju!

[words]
じゅうしょ | juusho | address
じゅぎょう | jugyou | class
じゅんび | junbi | preparation

[similar]
じ | ji | じゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. じ alone is just ji.

[distractors]
ja, jo
