title: Katakana GYO
kana: ギョ
script: katakana
romaji: gyo
sound: one quick sound, "gi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: gi + yo
mnemonic: ギ plus a small ョ makes ギョ. Say gi and yo together as one quick sound: gyo!

[words]
ギョーザ | gyooza | dumplings
ギョウザ | gyouza | dumplings

[similar]
ギ | gi | ギョ has a small ョ at the bottom right. ギ alone is just gi.

[distractors]
gya, gyu
