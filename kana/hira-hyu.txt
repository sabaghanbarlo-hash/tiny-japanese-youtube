title: Hiragana HYU
kana: ひゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: hyu
sound: one quick sound, "hi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: hi + yu
mnemonic: ひ plus a small ゅ makes ひゅ. Say hi and yu together as one quick sound: hyu!

[words]
ひゅっと | hyutto | whoosh
ひゅうひゅう | hyuuhyuu | whistling wind

[similar]
ひ | hi | ひゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. ひ alone is just hi.

[distractors]
hya, hyo
