title: Hiragana PA
kana: ぱ
script: hiragana
romaji: pa
key: pa
base: は
sound: like the "pa" in papa
mnemonic_word: ha to pa
mnemonic: は plus a little circle makes ぱ. The circle turns the h sound into a puff of air: ha becomes pa!

[words]
ぱん | pan | bread
ぱんだ | panda | panda
ぱぱ | papa | dad

[similar]
は | ha | ぱ has a small circle at the top right. は has none.
ば | ba | ぱ has a small circle. ば has two small marks.

[distractors]
ha, pi
