title: Hiragana GYA
kana: ぎゃ
script: hiragana
romaji: gya
sound: one quick sound, "gi" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: gi + ya
mnemonic: ぎ plus a small ゃ makes ぎゃ. Say gi and ya together as one quick sound: gya!

[words]
ぎゃく | gyaku | opposite
ぎゃくてん | gyakuten | reversal

[similar]
ぎ | gi | ぎゃ has a small ゃ at the bottom right. ぎ alone is just gi.

[distractors]
gyu, gyo
