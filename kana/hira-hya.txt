title: Hiragana HYA
kana: ひゃ
script: hiragana
romaji: hya
sound: one quick sound, "hi" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: hi + ya
mnemonic: ひ plus a small ゃ makes ひゃ. Say hi and ya together as one quick sound: hya!

[words]
ひゃく | hyaku | hundred
ひゃくえん | hyakuen | 100 yen
ひゃくにん | hyakunin | 100 people

[similar]
ひ | hi | ひゃ has a small ゃ at the bottom right. ひ alone is just hi.

[distractors]
hyu, hyo
