title: Katakana BYA
kana: ビャ
script: katakana
romaji: bya
sound: one quick sound, "bi" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: bi + ya
mnemonic: ビ plus a small ャ makes ビャ. Say bi and ya together as one quick sound: bya!

[words]
ビャクダン | byakudan | sandalwood
ビャッコ | byakko | white tiger

[similar]
ビ | bi | ビャ has a small ャ at the bottom right. ビ alone is just bi.

[distractors]
byu, byo
