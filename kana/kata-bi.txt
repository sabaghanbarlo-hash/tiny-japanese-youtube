title: Katakana BI
kana: ビ
script: katakana
romaji: bi
key: bi
base: ヒ
sound: like the "bee"
mnemonic_word: hi to bi
mnemonic: ヒ plus two little marks makes ビ. The marks make the sound buzz: hi turns into bi!

ビール | biiru | beer
ビル | biru | building
ビデオ | bideo | video

[similar]
ヒ | hi | ビ has two small marks at the top right. ヒ has none.

[distractors]
hi, bu
