title: Katakana KYU
kana: キュ
script: katakana
romaji: kyu
sound: one quick sound, "ki" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: ki + yu
mnemonic: キ plus a small ュ makes キュ. Say ki and yu together as one quick sound: kyu!

[words]
キュウリ | kyuuri | cucumber
キュート | kyuuto | cute
キューブ | kyuubu | cube

[similar]
キ | ki | キュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. キ alone is just ki.

[distractors]
kya, kyo
