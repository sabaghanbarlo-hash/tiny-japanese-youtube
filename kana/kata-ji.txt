title: Katakana JI
kana: ジ
script: katakana
romaji: ji
key: ji
base: シ
sound: like the "jee" in jeep
mnemonic_word: shi to ji
mnemonic: シ plus two little marks makes ジ. The marks make the sound buzz: shi turns into ji!

[words]
ジュース | juusu | juice
ジーンズ | jiinzu | jeans
ジム | jimu | gym

[similar]
シ | shi | ジ has two small marks at the top right. シ has none.
ヂ | ji | ジ and ヂ sound the same. ジ is used far more often.

[distractors]
shi, zu
