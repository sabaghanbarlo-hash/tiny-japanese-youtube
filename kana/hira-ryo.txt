title: Hiragana RYO
kana: りょ
script: hiragana
romaji: ryo
sound: one quick sound, "ri" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: ri + yo
mnemonic: り plus a small ょ makes りょ. Say ri and yo together as one quick sound: ryo!

[words]
りょこう | ryokou | travel
りょうり | ryouri | cooking
りょうしん | ryoushin | parents

[similar]
り | ri | りょ has a small ょ at the bottom right. り alone is just ri.

[distractors]
rya, ryu
