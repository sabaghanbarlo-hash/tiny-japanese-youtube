title: Hiragana RI
kana: り
script: hiragana
romaji: ri
sound: like the "ree" in reed
mnemonic_word: Reeds
mnemonic: り looks like two reeds by the river. Ri for reed!

りんご | ringo | apple
りす | risu | squirrel
くり | kuri | chestnut

[similar]
い | i | り has a longer stroke on the right. い has two strokes of similar height.
