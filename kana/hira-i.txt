title: Hiragana I
kana: い
script: hiragana
romaji: i
sound: like the "ee" in see
strokes: 2
mnemonic_word: Eels
mnemonic: い looks like two eels swimming side by side. Eels say ee!

[words]
いぬ | inu | dog
いし | ishi | stone
いえ | ie | house

[similar]
り | ri | い has two strokes of about the same height. り has a longer stroke on the right.

[distractors]
a, u
