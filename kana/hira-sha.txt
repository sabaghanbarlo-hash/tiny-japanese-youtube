title: Hiragana SHA
kana: しゃ
script: hiragana
romaji: sha
sound: one quick sound, "shi" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: shi + ya
mnemonic: し plus a small ゃ makes しゃ. Say shi and ya together as one quick sound: sha!

しゃかい | shakai | society
でんしゃ | densha | train

[similar]
