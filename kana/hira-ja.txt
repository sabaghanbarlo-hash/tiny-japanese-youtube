title: Hiragana JA
kana: じゃ
script: hiragana
romaji: ja
sound: one quick sound, "ji" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: ji + ya
mnemonic: じ plus a small ゃ makes じゃ. Say ji and ya together as one quick sound: ja!

[words]
じゃがいも | jagaimo | potato
じゃま | jama | obstacle
じゃんけん | janken | rock-paper-scissors

[similar]
じ | ji | じゃ has a small ゃ at the bottom right. じ alone is just ji.

[distractors]
ju, jo
