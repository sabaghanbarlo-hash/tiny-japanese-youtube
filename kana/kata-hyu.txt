title: Katakana HYU
kana: ヒュ
script: katakana
romaji: hyu
sound: one quick sound, "hi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: hi + yu
mnemonic: ヒ plus a small ュ makes ヒュ. Say hi and yu together as one quick sound: hyu!

[words]
ヒューズ | hyuuzu | fuse
ヒューマン | hyuuman | human
ヒュッテ | hyutte | mountain hut

[similar]
ヒ | hi | ヒュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. ヒ alone is just hi.

[distractors]
hya, hyo
