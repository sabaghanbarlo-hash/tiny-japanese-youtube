title: Hiragana KYA
kana: きゃ
script: hiragana
romaji: kya
sound: one quick sound, "ki" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: ki + ya
mnemonic: き plus a small ゃ makes きゃ. Say ki and ya together as one quick sound: kya!

[words]
きゃく | kyaku | guest
きゃべつ | kyabetsu | cabbage

[similar]
き | ki | きゃ has a small ゃ at the bottom right. き alone is just ki.

[distractors]
kyu, kyo
