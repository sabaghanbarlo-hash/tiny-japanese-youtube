title: Katakana SHU
kana: シュ
script: katakana
romaji: shu
sound: one quick sound, "shi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: shi + yu
mnemonic: シ plus a small ュ makes シュ. Say shi and yu together as one quick sound: shu!

[words]
シュート | shuuto | shot
シュークリーム | shuukuriimu | cream puff
シュガー | shugaa | sugar

[similar]
シ | shi | シュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. シ alone is just shi.

[distractors]
sha, sho
