title: Katakana NYA
kana: ニャ
script: katakana
romaji: nya
sound: one quick sound, "ni" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: ni + ya
mnemonic: ニ plus a small ャ makes ニャ. Say ni and ya together as one quick sound: nya!

[words]
ニャー | nyaa | meow
ニャンコ | nyanko | kitty
ニャン | nyan | meow

[similar]
ニ | ni | ニャ has a small ャ at the bottom right. ニ alone is just ni.

[distractors]
nyu, nyo
