title: Hiragana BE
kana: べ
script: hiragana
romaji: be
key: be
base: へ
sound: like the "be" in bed
mnemonic_word: he to be
mnemonic: へ plus two little marks makes べ. The marks make the sound buzz: he turns into be!

[words]
べんきょう | benkyou | study
べんとう | bentou | lunch box
たべる | taberu | to eat

[similar]
へ | he | べ has two small marks at the top right. へ has none.
