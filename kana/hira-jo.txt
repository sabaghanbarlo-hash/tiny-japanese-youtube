title: Hiragana JO
kana: じょ
script: hiragana
romaji: jo
sound: one quick sound, "ji" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: ji + yo
mnemonic: じ plus a small ょ makes じょ. Say ji and yo together as one quick sound: jo!

[words]
じょうず | jouzu | skilled
じょせい | josei | woman
じょうほう | jouhou | information

[similar]
じ | ji | じょ has a small ょ at the bottom right. じ alone is just ji.

[distractors]
ja, ju
