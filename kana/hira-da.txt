title: Hiragana DA
kana: だ
script: hiragana
romaji: da
key: da
base: た
sound: like the "da" in dart
mnemonic_word: ta to da
mnemonic: た plus two little marks makes だ. The marks make the sound buzz: ta turns into da!

[words]
だいがく | daigaku | university
だいどころ | daidokoro | kitchen
だれ | dare | who

[similar]
た | ta | だ has two small marks at the top right. た has none.

[distractors]
ta, de
