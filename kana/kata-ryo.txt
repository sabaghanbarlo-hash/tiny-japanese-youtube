title: Katakana RYO
kana: リョ
script: katakana
romaji: ryo
sound: one quick sound, "ri" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: ri + yo
mnemonic: リ plus a small ョ makes リョ. Say ri and yo together as one quick sound: ryo!

[words]
リョウリ | ryouri | cooking (katakana for emphasis)

[similar]
リ | ri | リョ has a small ョ at the bottom right. リ alone is just ri.

[distractors]
rya, ryu
