title: Hiragana BYU
kana: びゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: byu
sound: one quick sound, "bi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: bi + yu
mnemonic: び plus a small ゅ makes びゅ. Say bi and yu together as one quick sound: byu!

[words]
びゅうびゅう | byuubyuu | howling wind
びゅっと | byutto | whoosh

[similar]
び | bi | びゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. び alone is just bi.

[distractors]
bya, byo
