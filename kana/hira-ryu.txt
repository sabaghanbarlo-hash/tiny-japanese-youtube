title: Hiragana RYU
kana: りゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: ryu
sound: one quick sound, "ri" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: ri + yu
mnemonic: り plus a small ゅ makes りゅ. Say ri and yu together as one quick sound: ryu!

りゅうがく | ryuugaku | study abroad
りゅうこう | ryuukou | trend

[similar]
