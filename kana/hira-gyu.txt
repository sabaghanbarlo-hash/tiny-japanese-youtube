title: Hiragana GYU
kana: ぎゅ
script: hiragana
romaji: gyu
sound: one quick sound, "gi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: gi + yu
mnemonic: ぎ plus a small ゅ makes ぎゅ. Say gi and yu together as one quick sound: gyu!

[words]
ぎゅうにゅう | gyuunyuu | milk
ぎゅうにく | gyuuniku | beef
ぎゅうどん | gyuudon | beef bowl

[similar]
ぎ | gi | ぎゅ has a small ゅ at the bottom right. ぎ alone is just gi.

[distractors]
gya, gyo
