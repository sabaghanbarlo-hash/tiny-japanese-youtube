title: Katakana BYU
kana: ビュ
script: katakana
romaji: byu
sound: one quick sound, "bi" and "yu" blended
mnemonic_word: bi + yu
mnemonic: ビ plus a small ュ makes ビュ. Say bi and yu together as one quick sound: byu!

[words]
ビュッフェ | byuffe | buffet
ビューティー | byuutii | beauty
ビュー | byuu | view

[similar]
ビ | bi | ビュ has a small ュ at the bottom right. ビ alone is just bi.

[distractors]
bya, byo
