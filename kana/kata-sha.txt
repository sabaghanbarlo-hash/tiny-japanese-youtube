title: Katakana SHA
kana: シャ
script: katakana
romaji: sha
sound: one quick sound, "shi" and "ya" blended
mnemonic_word: shi + ya
mnemonic: シ plus a small ャ makes シャ. Say shi and ya together as one quick sound: sha!

[words]
シャツ | shatsu | shirt
シャワー | shawaa | shower
シャンプー | shanpuu | shampoo

[similar]
シ | shi | シャ has a small ャ at the bottom right. シ alone is just shi.

[distractors]
shu, sho
