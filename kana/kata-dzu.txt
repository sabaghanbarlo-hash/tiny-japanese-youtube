title: Katakana ZU (ヅ)
kana: ヅ
script: katakana
romaji: zu
key: dzu
base: ツ
sound: exactly like ズ (zu)
mnemonic_word: tsu to zu
mnemonic: ツ plus two little marks makes ヅ. The marks make the sound buzz: tsu turns into zu!

[words]
ヅラ | zura | wig (slang)
ヅ | zu | very rare spelling, same sound as ズ

[similar]
ツ | tsu | ヅ has two small marks at the top right. ツ has none.
ズ | zu | ヅ sounds exactly like ズ. ズ is used far more often.

[distractors]
da, ji
