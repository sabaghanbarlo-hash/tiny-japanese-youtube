title: Hiragana MYO
kana: みょ
script: hiragana
romaji: myo
sound: one quick sound, "mi" and "yo" blended
mnemonic_word: mi + yo
mnemonic: み plus a small ょ makes みょ. Say mi and yo together as one quick sound: myo!

[words]
みょうじ | myouji | family name
みょうにち | myounichi | tomorrow (formal)
みょうが | myouga | Japanese ginger

[similar]
み | mi | みょ has a small ょ at the bottom right. み alone is just mi.

[distractors]
mya, myu
