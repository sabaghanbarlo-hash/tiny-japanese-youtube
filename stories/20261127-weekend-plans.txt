[grammar]
grammar: Verb stem + ませんか | Use this to invite someone ("Would you like to ...?")
example: いっしょに いきませんか。 | Would you like to go together? | Issho ni ikimasen ka.
example: おちゃを のみませんか。 | Would you like to drink tea? | Ocha o nomimasen ka.
grammar: Verb て form + いく | Use this to say you take something along
example: おべんとうを もって いく。 | I take a lunch box. | Obentou o motte iku.
example: かさを もって いきます。 | I take an umbrella. | Kasa o motte ikimasu.

[quiz]
