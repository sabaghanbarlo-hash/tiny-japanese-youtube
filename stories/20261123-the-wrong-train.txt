title: The Wrong Train
scene: station

[story]
@time morning
@scene station
> Mika enters left
& Alex enters right
> Mika looks excited
Mika: きょうは かまくらに いくよ！ | Today we're going to Kamakura! | Kyou wa Kamakura ni iku yo!
> Alex looks happy
Alex: たのしみだな。 でんしゃは なんばんせん？ | I'm looking forward to it. Which platform is the train? | Tanoshimi da na. Densha wa nanbansen?
> Mika thinks
Mika: ええと……さんばんせん。 | Um... platform three. | Eeto... sanbansen.
@scene train
> Alex looks happy
Alex: ひさしぶりの でんしゃだ！ | It's been a while since I rode a train! | Hisashiburi no densha da!
> Mika looks surprised
Mika: ……あれ？ この でんしゃ、かまくらに いかない！ | ...Huh? This train doesn't go to Kamakura! | ...Are? Kono densha, Kamakura ni ikanai!
> Alex covers his mouth
Alex: ええっ！ まちがえた！ | What?! We made a mistake! | Ee! Machigaeta!
> Mika looks worried
Mika: つぎの えきで おりて、のりかえよう。 | Let's get off at the next station and change trains. | Tsugi no eki de orite, norikaeyou.
@scene station
> Hana enters right
Alex: すみません、かまくらに いきたいんですが……。 | Excuse me, I'd like to go to Kamakura... | Sumimasen, Kamakura ni ikitai n desu ga...
> Hana points right
Hana: かまくらですか。 いちばんせんから でんしゃが でますよ。 | Kamakura? A train leaves from platform one. | Kamakura desu ka. Ichibansen kara densha ga demasu yo.
> Mika bows
Mika: ありがとうございます！ | Thank you very much! | Arigatou gozaimasu!
> Hana looks smirking
Hana: じかんが あまり ありません。 はしって ください！ | There isn't much time. Please run! | Jikan ga amari arimasen. Hashitte kudasai!
> both run to 20
> Mika looks happy
Mika: ふう、まにあった！ | Phew, we made it! | Fuu, maniatta!
> Alex shrugs
Alex: つぎは ちゃんと かくにんしよう。 | Next time let's check properly. | Tsugi wa chanto kakunin shiyou.

[vocab]
vocab: でんしゃ | train | densha
example: でんしゃに のります。 | I ride the train. | Densha ni norimasu.
vocab: えき | station | eki
example: えきは ちかいです。 | The station is near. | Eki wa chikai desu.
vocab: のりかえる | to change trains | norikaeru
example: ここで のりかえます。 | I change trains here. | Koko de norikaemasu.
vocab: まちがえる | to make a mistake | machigaeru
example: みちを まちがえました。 | I took the wrong way. | Michi o machigaemashita.
vocab: おりる | to get off | oriru
example: つぎの えきで おります。 | I get off at the next station. | Tsugi no eki de orimasu.

[grammar]
grammar: Vehicle + に のる / Place + で おりる | Use these to say you get on a vehicle or get off at a place
example: でんしゃに のります。 | I get on the train. | Densha ni norimasu.
example: えきで おります。 | I get off at the station. | Eki de orimasu.
grammar: Verb stem + たいんですが | Use this to politely say "I'd like to ..."
example: かまくらに いきたいんですが。 | I'd like to go to Kamakura. | Kamakura ni ikitai n desu ga.
example: ちずを かいたいんですが。 | I'd like to buy a map. | Chizu o kaitai n desu ga.

[quiz]
quiz: What does のりかえる mean? | to get off | to change trains | to run | B | のりかえる means to change trains.
quiz: How do you say "I get off at the station"? | えきで おります | えきに おります | えきを おります | A | Place + で + おりる.
quiz: What does まちがえた mean? | made a mistake | arrived | forgot | A | まちがえる means to make a mistake.

[ending]
Mika and Alex: またね！ | See you again! | Mata ne!
