れんしゅう (Renshuu) - practice (練習)
かつ (Katsu) - to win (勝つ)
まける (Makeru) - to lose (負ける)
およぐ (Oyogu) - to swim (泳ぐ)

GRAMMAR
Saying You Like Something - Use が before 好きです to say what someone likes. Add か to make it a polite question.
