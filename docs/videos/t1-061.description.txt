VOCABULARY
こんげつ (Kongetsu) - this month (今月)
ことし (Kotoshi) - this year (今年)
こんしゅう (Konshuu) - this week (今週)
らいしゅう (Raishuu) - next week (来週)
まいつき (Maitsuki) - every month (毎月)
まいとし (Maitoshi) - every year (毎年)
なんがつ (Nangatsu) - what month (何月)
なんにち (Nannichi) - what day of the month (何日)
ねんまつ (Nenmatsu) - year-end (年末)
しゅくじつ (Shukujitsu) - national holiday; public holiday (祝日)

GRAMMAR
Asking About Dates - Use 何日に to ask what date an event happens. Use に after a date to say when an event happens.
