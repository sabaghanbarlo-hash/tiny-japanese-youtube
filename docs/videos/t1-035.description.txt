VOCABULARY
いつも (Itsumo) - always
よく (Yoku) - often
ときどき (Tokidoki) - sometimes (時々)
たまに (Tamani) - once in a while
あまり (Amari) - not very; not much
ぜんぜん (Zenzen) - not at all (全然)
かなり (Kanari) - quite; considerably
ちょっと (Chotto) - a little; a bit
すごく (Sugoku) - very; really
ほとんど (Hotondo) - almost; mostly

GRAMMAR
Adverbs Before Verbs - Frequency adverbs usually come before the verb they describe. Degree adverbs usually come before the adjective they describe.
