VOCABULARY
えっと (Etto) - um
あの (Ano) - um; excuse me
じゃあ (Jaa) - well then
けど (Kedo) - but; though
まあ (Maa) - well; I suppose
なんか (Nanka) - like; somehow
ところで (Tokorode) - by the way
つまり (Tsumari) - in other words
たとえば (Tatoeba) - for example (例えば)
まず (Mazu) - first; to begin with

GRAMMAR
Polite です Statements - です makes a statement polite. You can use it after nouns and adjectives.
