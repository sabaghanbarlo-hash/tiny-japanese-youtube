VOCABULARY
する (Suru) - to do
いく (Iku) - to go (行く)
くる (Kuru) - to come (来る)
みる (Miru) - to see, to watch (見る)
たべる (Taberu) - to eat (食べる)
のむ (Nomu) - to drink (飲む)
いう (Iu) - to say (言う)
おもう (Omou) - to think (思う)
しる (Shiru) - to know (知る)
わかる (Wakaru) - to understand (分かる)

GRAMMAR
Polite Questions with ますか - Use the polite verb ending ます for statements. Add か to make a polite question.
