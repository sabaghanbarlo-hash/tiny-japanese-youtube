VOCABULARY
ともだち (Tomodachi) - friend (友達)
せんせい (Sensei) - teacher (先生)
がくせい (Gakusei) - student (学生)
かぞく (Kazoku) - family (家族)
はは (Haha) - mother (my) (母)
ちち (Chichi) - father (my) (父)
おとこ (Otoko) - man (男)
おんな (Onna) - woman (女)
こども (Kodomo) - child (子供)
なまえ (Namae) - name (名前)

GRAMMAR
Topic は and polite です - Use は to mark the topic and です to make a polite statement. Add か at the end to make a polite yes-or-no question.
