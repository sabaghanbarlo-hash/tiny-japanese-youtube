VOCABULARY
どうして (Doushite) - why
どれ (Dore) - which one
いろいろ (Iroiro) - various; many kinds
きこえる (Kikoeru) - to be heard (聞こえる)
みんな (Minna) - everyone
まっすぐ (Massugu) - straight (真っ直ぐ)
やっと (Yatto) - finally
いつか (Itsuka) - someday
ひとり (Hitori) - one person; alone (一人)
ふたり (Futari) - two people (二人)

GRAMMAR
Polite Questions with か - Add か to the end of a sentence to make a polite question. The question word can come earlier in the sentence.
