VOCABULARY
おなじ (Onaji) - same (同じ)
より (Yori) - than
ほど (Hodo) - as much as; to the extent
いちばん (Ichiban) - most; number one (一番)
ずっと (Zutto) - continuously; all the time
くらべる (Kuraberu) - to compare (比べる)
にる (Niru) - to resemble (似る)
べつ (Betsu) - different; another (別)
さいこう (Saikou) - the best; highest (最高)
さいてい (Saitei) - the worst; lowest (最低)

GRAMMAR
Using より for comparisons - Put より after the thing you are comparing something to. It means “than” in English.
