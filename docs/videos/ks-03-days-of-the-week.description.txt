VOCABULARY
にちようび (Nichiyoubi) - Sunday (日曜日)
げつようび (Getsuyoubi) - Monday (月曜日)
かようび (Kayoubi) - Tuesday (火曜日)
すいようび (Suiyoubi) - Wednesday (水曜日)
もくようび (Mokuyoubi) - Thursday (木曜日)
きんようび (Kinyoubi) - Friday (金曜日)

GRAMMAR
The pattern - Each day is one kanji + 曜日 (ようび). 日 (nichi) Sunday, 月 (getsu) Monday, 火 (ka) Tuesday, 水 (sui) Wednesday, 木 (moku) Thursday, 金 (kin) Friday, 土 (do) Saturday. The on readings are the ones you hear.
Kanji with more than one job - 日 also means “day” and “sun” (ひ), 月 means “moon” and “month” (つき, がつ), 水 is “water” (みず), 金 is “gold” or “money” (かね), 木 is “tree” (き), 土 is “earth” (つち). So 一月 is January, and お金 is money.
