VOCABULARY
あげる (Ageru) - give
もらう (Morau) - receive
くれる (Kureru) - give (to me)
つくる (Tsukuru) - make (作る)
できる (Dekiru) - can do
やる (Yaru) - do
おくる (Okuru) - send (送る)
かす (Kasu) - lend (貸す)
かりる (Kariru) - borrow (借りる)
てつだう (Tetsudau) - help (手伝う)

GRAMMAR
Giving and Receiving - あげます means you give something to someone. もらいます means you receive something, and くれます means someone gives something to you.
