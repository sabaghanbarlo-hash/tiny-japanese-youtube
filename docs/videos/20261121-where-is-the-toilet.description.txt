How to Ask “Where Is the Toilet?” in Japanese | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

すみません、トイレは どこですか。 - Excuse me, where is the toilet?
あちらです。 - It's over there.
まっすぐ いって、ひだりです。 - Go straight, then it's on the left.
ひだりですね。ありがとう ございます！ - On the left. Thank you very much!

WORDS
トイレ (Toire) - toilet
ひだり (Hidari) - left
