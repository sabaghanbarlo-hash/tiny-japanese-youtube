VOCABULARY

せかい (Sekai) - world (世界)
にほん (Nihon) - Japan (日本)

しゃかい (Shakai) - society (社会)
せいふ (Seifu) - government (政府)
し (Shi) - city; municipality (市)
まち (Machi) - town (町)
けん (Ken) - prefecture (県)
ちいき (Chiiki) - region; area (地域)

GRAMMAR
は marks the topic - は marks the topic of a sentence. It is pronounced wa, even though it is written は.
