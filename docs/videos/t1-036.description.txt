VOCABULARY
きょねん (Kyonen) - last year (去年)
らいねん (Rainen) - next year (来年)
さっき (Sakki) - just now
あとで (Atode) - later
もうすぐ (Mousugu) - soon
さいきん (Saikin) - recently (最近)
ごぜん (Gozen) - morning; a.m. (午前)
ごご (Gogo) - afternoon; p.m. (午後)
こんど (Kondo) - next time; this time (今度)
さらいげつ (Saraigetsu) - the month after next (再来月)

GRAMMAR
Polite Past Tense - Use ました for a polite past-tense statement. Add か to make it a question.
