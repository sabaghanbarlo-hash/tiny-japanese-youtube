VOCABULARY
あさごはん (Asagohan) - breakfast (朝ご飯)
ひるごはん (Hirugohan) - lunch (昼ご飯)
ばんごはん (Bangohan) - dinner (晩ご飯)
コーヒー (Koohii) - coffee
しょくじ (Shokuji) - meal (食事)
おかし (Okashi) - snack; sweets (お菓子)
じゅうす (Juusu) - juice (ジュース)
こうちゃ (Koucha) - black tea (紅茶)
べんとう (Bentou) - boxed lunch (弁当)
おさけ (Osake) - alcohol; alcoholic drink (お酒)

GRAMMAR
Using を with food and drinks - Use を after the thing you eat or drink. 食べます and 飲みます are polite forms meaning “eat” and “drink.”
