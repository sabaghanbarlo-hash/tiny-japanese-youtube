VOCABULARY
ほしい (Hoshii) - want (something) (欲しい)
たい (Tai) - want to
ほしがる (Hoshigaru) - want (third person) (欲しがる)
かのう (Kanou) - possible (可能)
とくい (Tokui) - good at (得意)
にがて (Nigate) - not good at; dislike (苦手)
かもしれない (Kamoshirenai) - may; might
てもいい (Temoii) - may; be allowed to
なければならない (Nakerebanaranai) - must; have to
いる (Iru) - to need (要る)

GRAMMAR
が with Needs and Wants - Use が to mark the thing that is needed or wanted. 要りますか asks if it is needed, while 欲しいです says you want it.
