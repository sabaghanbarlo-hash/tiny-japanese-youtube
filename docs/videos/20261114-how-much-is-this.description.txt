How to Ask “How Much Is This?” in Japanese | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

いらっしゃいませ！ - Welcome!
すみません、これは いくらですか。 - Excuse me, how much is this?
それは ごひゃくえんです。 - That's 500 yen.
やすい！ これを おねがいします。 - Cheap! This one, please.
ありがとう ございます！ - Thank you very much!

WORDS
いくら (Ikura) - how much
ごひゃくえん (Gohyakuen) - 500 yen
