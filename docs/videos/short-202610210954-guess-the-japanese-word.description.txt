Guess the Japanese Word | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story, 1-2 key words and a quick quiz. Follow for a new lesson every day!

にほんご クイズ！この ことばの いみは なに？ - Japanese quiz! What does this word mean?
こうえん！ - Park!
A、classroom！ B、park！ C、café！ - A, classroom! B, park! C, café!
5びょうで こたえてね！ - Answer in five seconds!
5、4、3、2、1！ - Five, four, three, two, one!
こたえは B、park！ - The answer is B, park!
こうえんに いきます。 - I go to the park.
「こうえん」は park、「いきます」は go！ - “Kouen” means park, and “ikimasu” means go!
つぎの クイズも たのしみに してね！ - Look forward to the next quiz!
