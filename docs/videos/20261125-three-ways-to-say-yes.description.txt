3 Ways to Say “Yes” in Japanese | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

にほんごが すき？ - Do you like Japanese?
はい！ - Yes!
ていねいに いうと「はい」、やわらかく「ええ」。 - Polite: “hai.” Softer: “ee.”
ともだちには「うん」。 - With friends: “un.”
うん！ - Yeah!

WORDS
ええ (Ee) - yes (soft, polite)
うん (Un) - yeah (casual)
