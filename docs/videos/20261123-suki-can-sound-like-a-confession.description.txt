“Suki” Can Sound Like a Confession! | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

すきな たべものは？ - What food do you like?
すき！ - I like it!
なにが？ 「すき」だけだと こくはく みたいだよ！ - What? Just “suki” sounds like a confession!
えっ！ ラーメンが すき！ - Oh! I like ramen!
そう！「〜が すき」と いおう。 - Yes! Say “...ga suki.”

WORDS
すき (Suki) - like
だいすき (Daisuki) - love / like a lot
