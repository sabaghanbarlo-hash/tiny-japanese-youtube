3 Natural Japanese Phrases | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

アレックス、サッカーが すきなの？ - Alex, do you like soccer?
うん、だいすき！ - Yes, I love it!
そうなんだ！ - Oh, I see!
ミカは えを かくのが すき？ - Do you like drawing, Mika?
うん、すきだよ！ - Yes, I do!
いいね！ - That's nice!
ほんとう？ - Really?
うん、ほんとう！ - Yes, really!

WORDS
そうなんだ (Sounanda) - Oh, I see / Really
いいね (Iine) - That's nice / Sounds good
