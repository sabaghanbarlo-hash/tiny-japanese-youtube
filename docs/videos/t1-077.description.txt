VOCABULARY
こまる (Komaru) - to be troubled (困る)
こわれる (Kowareru) - to break (壊れる)
こわす (Kowasu) - to break something (壊す)
まちがえる (Machigaeru) - to get wrong (間違える)
おくれる (Okureru) - to be late; to be delayed (遅れる)
しっぱいする (Shippaisuru) - to fail; to make a mistake (失敗する)
おとす (Otosu) - to drop (落とす)
わすれもの (Wasuremono) - forgotten item (忘れ物)
めいわく (Meiwaku) - a nuisance; trouble for others (迷惑)
こしょうする (Koshousuru) - to break down (故障する)

GRAMMAR
Connecting with the て-form - The て-form can connect actions or describe what happens before another state. In this dialogue, 壊れて links the broken phone to Alex feeling troubled.
