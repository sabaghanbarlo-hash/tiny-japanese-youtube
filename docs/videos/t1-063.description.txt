VOCABULARY
やさしい (Yasashii) - kind, gentle (優しい)
きびしい (Kibishii) - strict (厳しい)
おもしろい (Omoshiroi) - funny, interesting (面白い)
しずか (Shizuka) - quiet (静か)
あかるい (Akarui) - bright; cheerful (明るい)
まじめ (Majime) - serious, diligent (真面目)
かわいい (Kawaii) - cute
きれい (Kirei) - beautiful
しんせつ (Shinsetsu) - kind, helpful (親切)
かしこい (Kashikoi) - clever; intelligent (賢い)

GRAMMAR
な-Adjectives Before Nouns - な-adjectives use な before a noun. 静か and きれい are both な-adjectives.
