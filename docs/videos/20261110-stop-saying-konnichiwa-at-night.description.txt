Stop Saying “Konnichiwa” at Night! | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

こんにちは！ - Hello!
アレックス、いまは よるだよ。 - Alex, it's night now.
よるは「こんばんは」。 - At night: “konbanwa.”
こんばんは！ - Good evening!
そう！ あさは「おはよう」。 - Yes! In the morning: “ohayou.”

WORDS
こんばんは (Konbanha) - good evening
おはよう (Ohayou) - good morning
