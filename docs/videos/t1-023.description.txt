VOCABULARY
へや (Heya) - room (部屋)
だいどころ (Daidokoro) - kitchen (台所)
つくえ (Tsukue) - desk (机)
いす (Isu) - chair (椅子)
まど (Mado) - window (窓)
どあ (Doa) - door (ドア)
べっど (Beddo) - bed (ベッド)
てーぶる (Teeburu) - table (テーブル)
てれび (Terebi) - TV (テレビ)
れいぞうこ (Reizouko) - refrigerator (冷蔵庫)

GRAMMAR
あります for Things - Use あります to say that an inanimate thing exists or is located somewhere. Put the place before に and the thing before が.
