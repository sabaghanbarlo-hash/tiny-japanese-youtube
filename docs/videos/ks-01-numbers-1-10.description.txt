VOCABULARY
ひとり (Hitori) - one person (一人)
ふたり (Futari) - two people (二人)
さんにん (Sannin) - three people (三人)
ごがつ (Gogatsu) - May (五月)
じゅうがつ (Juugatsu) - October (十月)
よじ (Yoji) - 4 o'clock (四時)

GRAMMAR
Two readings for 4, 7 and 9 - 四 is よん or し, 七 is なな or しち, 九 is きゅう or く. Counting uses よん, なな and きゅう. But 4 o'clock, 7 o'clock and 9 o'clock use the older readings: よじ, しちじ, くじ.
Numbers combine by position - 十 is ten, so 二十 is two tens (20), 三十 is thirty and 十一 is eleven (ten + one). The kanji add up like arithmetic: 十二 = 12.
