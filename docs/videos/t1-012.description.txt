VOCABULARY
おきる (Okiru) - wake up (起きる)
ねる (Neru) - sleep; go to bed (寝る)
あらう (Arau) - wash (洗う)
かえる (Kaeru) - return home (帰る)
はいる (Hairu) - enter; go in (入る)
あびる (Abiru) - take (a shower) (浴びる)
きる (Kiru) - put on; wear (着る)
ぬぐ (Nugu) - take off (clothes) (脱ぐ)
みがく (Migaku) - brush; polish (磨く)
でる (Deru) - leave; go out (出る)

GRAMMAR
Linking Actions with て - The て-form can link actions that happen in sequence. The final verb gives the sentence its polite ending.
