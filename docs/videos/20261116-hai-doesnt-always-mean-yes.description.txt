“Hai” Doesn’t Always Mean “Yes” in Japanese | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

アレックス、わかった？ - Alex, did you get it?
はい、はい！ - Hai, hai!
ほんとうに？ - Really?
…じつは、わかりません。 - ...Actually, I don't understand.
「はい」は「きいてるよ」の ときも あるよ。 - Sometimes “hai” means “I'm listening.”
なるほど！ - I see!

WORDS
はい (Hai) - yes / I'm listening
わかりません (Wakarimasen) - I don't understand
