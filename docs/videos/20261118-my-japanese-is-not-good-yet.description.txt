How to Say “My Japanese Is Not Good Yet” | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

いらっしゃいませ！ なにに しますか？ - Welcome! What would you like?
すみません、にほんごは まだ じょうずじゃ ありません。 - Sorry, my Japanese isn't good yet.
だいじょうぶ ですよ！ - That's okay!
ゆっくり はなしますね。 - I'll speak slowly.
ありがとう ございます！ - Thank you very much!

WORDS
まだ (Mada) - not yet / still
ゆっくり (Yukkuri) - slowly
