VOCABULARY
きょう (Kyou) - today (今日)
あした (Ashita) - tomorrow (明日)
きのう (Kinou) - yesterday (昨日)
いま (Ima) - now (今)
じかん (Jikan) - time (時間)
あさ (Asa) - morning (朝)
よる (Yoru) - night (夜)
まいにち (Mainichi) - every day (毎日)
しゅう (Shuu) - week (週)
まえ (Mae) - before; in front (前)

GRAMMAR
は Marks the Topic - は marks the topic of a sentence. It often comes after the person or thing being talked about.
