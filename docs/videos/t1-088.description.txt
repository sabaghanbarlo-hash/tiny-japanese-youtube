VOCABULARY
うるさい (Urusai) - noisy; annoying
まずい (Mazui) - bad-tasting
ひくい (Hikui) - low (低い)
べんり (Benri) - convenient (便利)
ゆうめい (Yuumei) - famous (有名)
ひま (Hima) - free; not busy (暇)
かんたん (Kantan) - easy; simple (簡単)
たいへん (Taihen) - tough; hard (大変)
せまい (Semai) - narrow; cramped (狭い)
ひろい (Hiroi) - wide; spacious (広い)

GRAMMAR
Adjectives with です - Put です after an adjective to make a polite statement. Both い-adjectives and な-adjectives can be used this way.
