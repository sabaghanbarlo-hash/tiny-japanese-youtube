VOCABULARY
ここ (Koko) - here
そこ (Soko) - there
どこ (Doko) - where
だれ (Dare) - who (誰)
いつ (Itsu) - when
どう (Dou) - how
この (Kono) - this (+noun)
その (Sono) - that (+noun)
ありがとう (Arigatou) - thank you
すみません (Sumimasen) - excuse me; sorry

GRAMMAR
この and その - この means “this” and comes before a noun. その means “that” and also comes before a noun.
