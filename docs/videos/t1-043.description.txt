VOCABULARY
かいぎ (Kaigi) - meeting (会議)
メール (Meeru) - email
しょるい (Shorui) - documents; paperwork (書類)
どうりょう (Douryou) - coworker (同僚)
めいし (Meishi) - business card (名刺)
かいぎしつ (Kaigishitsu) - meeting room (会議室)
ざんぎょうする (Zangyousuru) - to work overtime (残業する)
じむしょ (Jimusho) - office (事務所)
しりょう (Shiryou) - materials; documents (資料)
ぶちょう (Buchou) - department manager (部長)

GRAMMAR
Asking Where Something Is - Use は to mark the thing you are asking about. Add どこ ですか to ask where it is.
