VOCABULARY
かんがえる (Kangaeru) - to think; consider (考える)
いけん (Iken) - opinion (意見)
たぶん (Tabun) - probably (多分)
ほんとう (Hontou) - true; real (本当)
もちろん (Mochiron) - of course
そう (Sou) - so; that way
ちがう (Chigau) - to differ; be wrong (違う)
しんじる (Shinjiru) - to believe (信じる)
きめる (Kimeru) - to decide (決める)
りゆう (Riyuu) - reason (理由)

GRAMMAR
Sharing What You Think - Put と 思います after a thought to say “I think.” The thought comes before と.
