VOCABULARY
それから (Sorekara) - after that; and then
それに (Soreni) - besides; moreover
ただ (Tada) - however; just
やっぱり (Yappari) - after all; as expected
たいてい (Taitei) - usually
だいたい (Daitai) - usually; roughly
それとも (Soretomo) - or
じつは (Jitsuha) - actually; to tell the truth (実は)
とくに (Tokuni) - especially; particularly (特に)
しかし (Shikashi) - however; but

GRAMMAR
Using それから - それから connects actions that happen in order. It means “after that” or “and then.”
