VOCABULARY
チェックイン (Chekkuin) - check-in / check-out
よやく (Yoyaku) - reservation
はく (Haku) - nights (にはく 2 nights)
あさごはん (Asagohan) - breakfast
から まで (Kara made) - from ~ to ~
パスワード (Pasuwaado) - password

GRAMMAR
から and まで for ranges - から means “from” and まで means “until”: しちじから くじまで (from seven to nine). Use them for opening hours, breakfast times and check-out times.
Counting nights and floors - Add はく for nights: いっぱく (1), にはく (2), さんぱく (3). Add かい for floors: いっかい (1st), にかい (2nd), ごかい (5th). Some counters change their sound, so learn them as they come.
