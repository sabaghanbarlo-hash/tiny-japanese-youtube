VOCABULARY
かう (Kau) - to buy (買う)
はなす (Hanasu) - to speak (話す)
きく (Kiku) - to listen; to ask (聞く)
よむ (Yomu) - to read (読む)
かく (Kaku) - to write (書く)
つかう (Tsukau) - to use (使う)
まつ (Matsu) - to wait (待つ)
もつ (Motsu) - to hold; to have (持つ)
すむ (Sumu) - to live (reside) (住む)
はたらく (Hataraku) - to work (働く)

GRAMMAR
〜ています for states - Use 〜ています to describe a continuing situation or state. It can tell where someone lives or what they have.
