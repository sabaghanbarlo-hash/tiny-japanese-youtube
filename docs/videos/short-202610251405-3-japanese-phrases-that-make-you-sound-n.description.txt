そうなんだ！ - Oh, really! / I see!

ほんとう？ - Really?
ともだちとの かいわで つかえるよ！ - You can use these in conversations with friends!
アレックスは サッカーが すきなんだ。 - So, you like soccer, Alex.
そうなんだ！ミカは？ - That's right! What about you, Mika?
わたしは えを かくのが すき！ - I like drawing!
いいね！ - That's nice!
「ほんとう？」は えいごで なに？ - What does “hontou?” mean in English?
A、Really? B、Goodbye. C、Thank you. - A, Really? B, Goodbye. C, Thank you.
こたえは A！ - The answer is A!
