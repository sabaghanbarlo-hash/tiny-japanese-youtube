How to Say I'm Hungry in Japanese | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

ミカ、おなかが すいた！ - Mika, I'm hungry!
わたしも！なにを たべたい？ - Me too! What do you want to eat?
サンドイッチを たべたい！ - I want to eat a sandwich!
いいね！わたしは おにぎりが いいな。 - Sounds good! I'd like an onigiri.
おなかが すいた とき、たべものが ほしくなるね。 - When we're hungry, we want food.
そうだね！ - That's right!
じゃあ、たべに いこう！ - Then let's go eat!
うん、いこう！ - Yes, let's go!

WORDS
おなか (Onaka) - stomach
たべる (Taberu) - to eat
