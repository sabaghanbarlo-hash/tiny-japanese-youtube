VOCABULARY
しゅみ (shumi) - hobby
おんがく (ongaku) - music
え (e) - picture; drawing
かく (kaku) - to draw; to write
サッカー (sakkaa) - soccer
こうえん (kouen) - park
さむい (samui) - cold (weather)
きょく (kyoku) - song; musical piece
どんな (donna) - what kind of
たのしみ (tanoshimi) - something to look forward to; anticipation

GRAMMAR
Noun が すきです - Use this to say you like something.
Noun は？ - Use this shortened question to ask “What about...?” when the rest is understood.
どんな + noun - Use どんな to ask what kind of something a person likes or wants.
Verb dictionary form + ことが すきです - Use this pattern to say you like doing an activity.
Verb ましょう / よう - These are ways to suggest doing something together. ましょう is polite; よう is a casual form.
