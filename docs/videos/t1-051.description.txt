VOCABULARY
おや (Oya) - parent (親)
かれし (Kareshi) - boyfriend (彼氏)
かのじょ (Kanojo) - girlfriend (彼女)
こいびと (Koibito) - romantic partner (恋人)
むすこ (Musuko) - son (息子)
むすめ (Musume) - daughter (娘)
きょうだい (Kyoudai) - siblings (兄弟)
いとこ (Itoko) - cousin
しんゆう (Shinyuu) - best friend (親友)
ふうふ (Fuufu) - married couple (夫婦)

GRAMMAR
Using も for “also” - も means “also” and comes after the word or phrase it adds. It lets the speaker add another person or fact without repeating the whole idea.
