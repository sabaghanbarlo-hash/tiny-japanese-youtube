“Itadakimasu” Does NOT Mean “Thank You” | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

いただきます！ ありがとう！ - Itadakimasu! Thank you!
いただきますは「ありがとう」じゃ ないよ。 - Itadakimasu isn't “thank you.”
たべる まえに いうよ。 - We say it before eating.
おわったら？ - And when I'm finished?
ごちそうさまでした！ - Gochisousama deshita!

WORDS
いただきます (Itadakimasu) - said before eating
ごちそうさまでした (Gochisousamadeshita) - said after eating
