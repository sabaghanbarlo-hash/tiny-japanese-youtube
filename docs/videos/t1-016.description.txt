VOCABULARY
みぎ (Migi) - right (右)
ひだり (Hidari) - left (左)
うえ (Ue) - above; on top (上)
した (Shita) - below; under (下)
なか (Naka) - inside (中)
そと (Soto) - outside (外)
となり (Tonari) - next to; neighboring (隣)
うしろ (Ushiro) - behind; back (後ろ)
ちかく (Chikaku) - near; nearby (近く)
よこ (Yoko) - beside; side (横)

GRAMMAR
Using の With Positions - Put の between a place and a position word to show where something is. The pattern is [thing] は [place] の [position] です.
