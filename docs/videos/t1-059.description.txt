VOCABULARY
まんなか (Mannaka) - center; middle (真ん中)
かど (Kado) - corner (角)
すみ (Sumi) - corner; nook (隅)
はし (Hashi) - edge; end (端)
おく (Oku) - back; inner part (奥)
てまえ (Temae) - this side; just before (手前)
むこう (Mukou) - other side; over there (向こう)
あいだ (Aida) - between (間)
がわ (Gawa) - side (側)
そば (Soba) - near; beside

GRAMMAR
Saying Where Things Are - Use にあります to say where something is. Put the place before にあります.
