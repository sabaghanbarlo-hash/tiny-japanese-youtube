3 Ways to Say “Delicious!” in Japanese | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

たべて みて！ - Try it!
おいしい！ - Delicious!
ともだちには「うまい！」とも いうよ。 - With friends you can also say “umai!”
めちゃくちゃ おいしい！ - Super delicious!
すごい、すごい！ - Awesome!

WORDS
おいしい (Oishii) - delicious
うまい (Umai) - tasty (casual)
