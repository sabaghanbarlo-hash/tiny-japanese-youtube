How to Say Thank You Politely | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story, 1-2 key words and a quick quiz. Follow for a new lesson every day!

アレックス、ありがとう は しってる？ - Alex, do you know how to say thank you?
うん！ありがとう！ - Yes! Thank you!
でも、もっと ていねいな いいかた が あるよ！ - But there's a more polite way to say it!
えっ、なに？ - Huh, what is it?
ありがとうございます！ - Thank you very much!
ありがとうございます！これで いい？ - Thank you very much! Is this right?
ていねいに いって みて！ - Try saying it politely!
あなたの ばん！ - Your turn!
どちらが ていねい？ - Which one is polite?
A、こんにちは B、ありがとうございます C、おやすみ - A, Hello B, Thank you very much C, Good night
こたえは B！ありがとうございます！ - The answer is B! Thank you very much!
ほかの ありがとう も しってる？コメントしてね！ - Do you know other ways to say thank you? Comment below!
またね！ - See you!
