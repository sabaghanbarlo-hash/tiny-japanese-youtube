VOCABULARY
かかる (Kakaru) - take (time)
いそぐ (Isogu) - hurry (急ぐ)
うる (Uru) - to sell (売る)
おす (Osu) - to push (押す)
ひく (Hiku) - to pull (引く)
なく (Naku) - to cry (泣く)
ふる (Furu) - to fall (rain or snow) (降る)
しまる (Shimaru) - to close (閉まる)
あく (Aku) - to open (開く)
にあう (Niau) - to suit; to look good on (似合う)

GRAMMAR
Polite verb forms - In polite speech, verbs often end in ます. Add か to make a polite question.
