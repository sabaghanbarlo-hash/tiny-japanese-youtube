VOCABULARY
いえ (Ie) - house; home (家)
がっこう (Gakkou) - school (学校)
かいしゃ (Kaisha) - company (会社)
えき (Eki) - station (駅)
みせ (Mise) - shop (店)
みず (Mizu) - water (水)
おちゃ (Ocha) - tea (お茶)
ほん (Hon) - book (本)
くるま (Kuruma) - car (車)
でんわ (Denwa) - phone; call (電話)

GRAMMAR
あります for Things - Use あります to say that something exists or is located somewhere. Put the place before に and the thing before が.
