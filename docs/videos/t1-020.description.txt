VOCABULARY
あつい (Atsui) - hot (to the touch) (熱い)
つめたい (Tsumetai) - cold (to the touch) (冷たい)
ながい (Nagai) - long (長い)
みじかい (Mijikai) - short (短い)
はやい (Hayai) - early (早い)
おそい (Osoi) - late; slow (遅い)
とおい (Tooi) - far (遠い)
ちかい (Chikai) - near (近い)
はやい (Hayai) - fast (速い)
あさい (Asai) - shallow (浅い)

GRAMMAR
Polite adjective sentences - Use an い-adjective with です to make a polite statement. Add か to make a polite question.
