VOCABULARY
すぐ (Sugu) - right away; soon
ゆっくり (Yukkuri) - slowly; at your leisure
きっと (Kitto) - surely; probably
ちゃんと (Chanto) - properly; reliably
だんだん (Dandan) - gradually
そろそろ (Sorosoro) - soon; about time
たいてい (Taitei) - usually; generally (大抵)
もし (Moshi) - if
しっかり (Shikkari) - firmly; thoroughly
はっきり (Hakkiri) - clearly

GRAMMAR
Adverbs Before Verbs - Japanese adverbs often come before the verbs they describe. They tell you how an action is done.
