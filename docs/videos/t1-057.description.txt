VOCABULARY
あける (Akeru) - open (開ける)
しめる (Shimeru) - close (閉める)
おく (Oku) - put; place (置く)
とる (Toru) - take; get (取る)
いれる (Ireru) - put in (入れる)
だす (Dasu) - take out (出す)
つける (Tsukeru) - turn on
けす (Kesu) - turn off (消す)
はこぶ (Hakobu) - carry (運ぶ)
とどける (Todokeru) - deliver; bring (届ける)

GRAMMAR
Polite Yes-or-No Questions - Add か to a polite ます-form verb to make a yes-or-no question. The dialogue uses this pattern to ask what Alex will do.
