“Chotto” Can Mean NO in Japanese (Polite Refusal) | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

あした、いっしょに ごはん たべる？ - Want to eat together tomorrow?
あしたは ちょっと… - Tomorrow is a little...
ちょっと なに？ - A little what?
「ちょっと…」は やわらかい「ノー」だよ。 - “Chotto...” is a soft “no.”
あ、わかった！ - Oh, I get it!
また こんど ね！ - Next time!

WORDS
ちょっと (Chotto) - a little / (soft no)
こんど (Kondo) - next time
