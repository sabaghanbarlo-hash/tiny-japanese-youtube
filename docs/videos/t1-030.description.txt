VOCABULARY
つ (Tsu) - general counter for things
まい (Mai) - counter for flat objects (枚)
さつ (Satsu) - counter for books (冊)
だい (Dai) - counter for machines (台)
ひき (Hiki) - counter for small animals (匹)
じ (Ji) - o'clock (時)
ふん (Fun) - minute counter (分)
めい (Mei) - counter for people (名)
かい (Kai) - counter for times (回)
さい (Sai) - years old (age counter) (歳)

GRAMMAR
Numbers and Counters - For many counters, place the counter after the number. In questions, 何 (なん) comes before the counter.
