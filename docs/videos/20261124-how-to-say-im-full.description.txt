How to Say “I’m Full!” in Japanese | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

もっと たべる？ - Want more?
もう おなかが いっぱい！ - I'm already full!
デザートは？ - What about dessert?
デザートは べつばら！ - Dessert goes in a separate stomach!
ふふ、わたしも！ - Ha, me too!

WORDS
おなかが いっぱい (Onakaga ippai) - full (stomach)
もっと (Motto) - more
