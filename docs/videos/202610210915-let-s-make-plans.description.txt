GRAMMAR
Verb ない-form + ？ - Use the casual negative form of a verb to make a casual invitation or suggestion.
Time + は どう？ - Use this to suggest a time.
Place + で + action - Use で to mark the place where an action happens.
Verb たいです - Add たいです to a verb stem to say what you want to do.
Noun を ひとつ おねがいします - A useful way to order one item politely. ひとつ means one item.
