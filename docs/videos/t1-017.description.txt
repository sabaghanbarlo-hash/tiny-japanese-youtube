VOCABULARY
てんき (Tenki) - weather (天気)
あめ (Ame) - rain (雨)
はれる (Hareru) - to clear up (晴れる)
くもる (Kumoru) - to become cloudy (曇る)
あつい (Atsui) - hot (weather) (暑い)
さむい (Samui) - cold (weather) (寒い)
はる (Haru) - spring (春)
なつ (Natsu) - summer (夏)
あき (Aki) - autumn (秋)
ふゆ (Fuyu) - winter (冬)

GRAMMAR
い-Adjectives with です - Use い-adjectives before です to make polite descriptions. The adjective keeps its い ending.
