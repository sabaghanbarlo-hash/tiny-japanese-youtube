VOCABULARY
わたし (Watashi) - I, me
さん (San) - Mr, Ms (added to names)
がくせい (Gakusei) - student
こうこうせい (Koukousei) - high school student
せんせい (Sensei) - teacher
かいしゃいん (Kaishain) - office worker

GRAMMAR
A は B です - Put the topic first, then は, then the noun, then です. です is the polite “is / am / are”. Notice that は is read “wa” when it is a particle: わたしは がくせい です (Watashi wa gakusei desu).
か turns it into a question - Add か at the end: にほんじん ですか。 There is no need for a question mark, and your voice usually rises a little. Answer with はい (yes) or いいえ (no).
