VOCABULARY
かえす (Kaesu) - return (something) (返す)
もどる (Modoru) - return; go back (戻る)
うけとる (Uketoru) - receive; accept (受け取る)
とどく (Todoku) - arrive; be delivered (届く)
わたす (Watasu) - hand; pass (渡す)
あずかる (Azukaru) - keep; look after (預かる)
むかえる (Mukaeru) - meet; pick up (迎える)
かける (Kakeru) - hang; put on
おりかえす (Orikaesu) - call back (折り返す)
へんしんする (Henshinsuru) - reply (返信する)

GRAMMAR
Polite Past Questions - Use the polite past form ましたか to ask whether something happened. Add か after the polite past verb to make a question.
