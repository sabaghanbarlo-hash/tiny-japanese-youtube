VOCABULARY
ほうちょう (Houchou) - kitchen knife (包丁)
なべ (Nabe) - pot (鍋)
フライパン (Furaipan) - frying pan
しお (Shio) - salt (塩)
さとう (Satou) - sugar (砂糖)
あぶら (Abura) - oil (油)
やく (Yaku) - to grill; to bake (焼く)
まぜる (Mazeru) - to mix (混ぜる)
きる (Kiru) - to cut (切る)
まないた (Manaita) - cutting board (まな板)

GRAMMAR
The object marker を - を marks the thing that receives an action. It often comes before the verb that describes the action.
