VOCABULARY
りょうりする (Ryourisuru) - to cook (料理する)
かわかす (Kawakasu) - to dry something (乾かす)
たたむ (Tatamu) - to fold (畳む)
しまう (Shimau) - to put away
わかす (Wakasu) - to boil (沸かす)
あたためる (Atatameru) - to warm something (温める)
ひやす (Hiyasu) - to chill something (冷やす)
ながす (Nagasu) - to run or flush water (流す)
したくする (Shitakusuru) - to get ready (支度する)
とかす (Tokasu) - to comb

GRAMMAR
Connecting Actions with て-Form - Use the て-form to connect actions done in sequence. The final verb shows what happens last.
