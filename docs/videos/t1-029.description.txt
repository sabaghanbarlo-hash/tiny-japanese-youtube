VOCABULARY
しゅみ (Shumi) - hobby (趣味)
おんがく (Ongaku) - music (音楽)
えいが (Eiga) - movie; film (映画)
スポーツ (Supootsu) - sports
ゲーム (Geemu) - game
りょこう (Ryokou) - travel; trip (旅行)
しゃしん (Shashin) - photo (写真)
うたう (Utau) - to sing (歌う)
あそぶ (Asobu) - to play; to hang out (遊ぶ)
やすむ (Yasumu) - to rest; to take time off (休む)

GRAMMAR
Polite Likes with 好きです - Use 好きです after a thing to say you like it. Add か to make it a polite question.
