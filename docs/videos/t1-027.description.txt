VOCABULARY
あるく (Aruku) - to walk (歩く)
はしる (Hashiru) - to run (走る)
つく (Tsuku) - to arrive (着く)
まがる (Magaru) - to turn (曲がる)
わたる (Wataru) - to cross (渡る)
すすむ (Susumu) - to move forward; to proceed (進む)
とおる (Tooru) - to pass through; to go along (通る)
むかう (Mukau) - to head toward (向かう)
よる (Yoru) - to stop by (寄る)
ちかづく (Chikazuku) - to approach; to get closer (近づく)

GRAMMAR
Connecting Actions with て - The て-form connects actions in order. It often means “and then” in simple directions.
