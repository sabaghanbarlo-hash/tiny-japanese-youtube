VOCABULARY
ぶんか (Bunka) - culture (文化)
じんじゃ (Jinja) - Shinto shrine (神社)
おてら (Otera) - Buddhist temple (お寺)
きもの (Kimono) - kimono (着物)
わしょく (Washoku) - Japanese food (和食)
おんせん (Onsen) - hot spring (温泉)
まんが (Manga) - manga (漫画)
でんとう (Dentou) - tradition (伝統)
れきし (Rekishi) - history (歴史)
きょうと (Kyouto) - Kyoto (京都)

GRAMMAR
〜が好きです - Use が before 好きです to say what you like. You can use this pattern to talk about people, places, or things.
