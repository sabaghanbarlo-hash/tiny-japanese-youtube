VOCABULARY
おおい (Ooi) - many; a lot (多い)
すくない (Sukunai) - few; little (少ない)
ぜんぶ (Zenbu) - all; everything (全部)
はんぶん (Hanbun) - half (半分)
いくつ (Ikutsu) - how many
いくら (Ikura) - how much
たりる (Tariru) - to be enough (足りる)
あまる (Amaru) - to be left over (余る)
ちょうど (Choudo) - exactly; just
いっぱい (Ippai) - a lot; full

GRAMMAR
Asking About Quantity - Use いくつ to ask how many items someone will buy. Use いくら to ask how much money someone has.
