3 Tiny Japanese Words | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story, 1-2 key words and a quick quiz. Follow for a new lesson every day!

きょうは、みじかい にほんごを みっつ！ - Today, three short Japanese expressions!
はい！ - Yes!
いいえ！ - No!
ありがとう！ - Thank you!
「はい」は yes！ - “Hai” means yes!
「いいえ」は no！ - “Iie” means no!
「ありがとう」は thank you！ - “Arigatou” means thank you!
じゃあ、クイズ！「Thank you」は どれ？ - Now, a quiz! Which one means “thank you”?
A、はい！ B、いいえ！ C、ありがとう！ - A, yes! B, no! C, thank you!
こたえは C！ありがとう！ - The answer is C! Arigatou!
また らいしゅう！ - See you next week!
