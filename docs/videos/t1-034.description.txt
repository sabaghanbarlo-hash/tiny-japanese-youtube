VOCABULARY
から (Kara) - from; because
まで (Made) - until; up to
だけ (Dake) - only; just
と (To) - with; and
で (De) - by; with; at
について (Nitsuite) - about; concerning
ので (Node) - because; since
しか (Shika) - only (with a negative)
くらい (Kurai) - about; approximately
も (Mo) - also; too

GRAMMAR
から for where someone is from - から can mark the place someone comes from. It comes after the place or question word.
