GRAMMAR
の links two nouns - A の B means “A's B” or “the B of A”: わたしの かばん (my bag), ミカさんの ほん (Mika's book), にほんごの ほん (a Japanese book). The order is the opposite of “book of Mika”: owner first.
の can replace the noun - When the noun is obvious you can drop it and keep の: それは アレックスさんの です。 means “That is Alex's.”
