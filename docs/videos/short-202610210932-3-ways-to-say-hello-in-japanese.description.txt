3 Ways to Say Hello in Japanese | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story, 1-2 key words and a quick quiz. Follow for a new lesson every day!

にほんごで「Hello」って なんていうの？ - How do you say “hello” in Japanese?
じつは、いろいろ あるよ！ - Actually, there are several ways!
おはよう！ - Good morning! (casual)
おはようございます！ - Good morning! (polite)
こんにちは！ - Hello! / Good afternoon!
こんばんは！ - Good evening!
ともだちには「おはよう」！ - Say “ohayou” to friends!
ていねいに はなすときは「おはようございます」！ - Use “ohayou gozaimasu” when speaking politely!
ひるの あいさつは どれ？ - Which greeting do you use during the day?
A、おはよう！ B、こんにちは！ C、こんばんは！ - A, good morning! B, hello! C, good evening!
こたえは B！こんにちは！ - The answer is B! Konnichiwa!
どれか ひとつ、コメントに かいてね！ - Write one of these in the comments!
またね！ - See you!
