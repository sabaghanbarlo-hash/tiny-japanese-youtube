VOCABULARY
でんしゃ (densha) - train
えき (eki) - station
のりかえる (norikaeru) - to change trains
まちがえる (machigaeru) - to make a mistake
おりる (oriru) - to get off

GRAMMAR
Vehicle + に のる / Place + で おりる - Use these to say you get on a vehicle or get off at a place
Verb stem + たいんですが - Use this to politely say "I'd like to ..."
