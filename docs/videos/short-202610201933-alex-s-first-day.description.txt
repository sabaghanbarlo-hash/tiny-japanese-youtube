Alex's First Day | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story, 1-2 key words and a quick quiz. Follow for a new lesson every day!

おはよう、アレックス！ - Good morning, Alex!
おはよう。きょうは がっこうの はじめての ひだね。 - Morning. Today is my first day at school.
いっしょに いこう！ - Let's go together!
ここが きょうしつ？ - Is this the classroom?
そう。わたしの となりに すわって。 - Yes. Sit next to me.

WORDS
きょうしつ (Kyoushitsu) - classroom
となり (Tonari) - next to
