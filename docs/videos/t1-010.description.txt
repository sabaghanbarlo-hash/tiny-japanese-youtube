VOCABULARY
とても (Totemo) - very
もっと (Motto) - more
すこし (Sukoshi) - a little (少し)
たくさん (Takusan) - a lot
でも (Demo) - but
そして (Soshite) - and then
だから (Dakara) - so, therefore
また (Mata) - again
まだ (Mada) - still; not yet
もう (Mou) - already; more

GRAMMAR
Using でも for contrast - でも means “but” and often starts a sentence that contrasts with the idea before it. In polite conversation, it can connect two simple statements.
