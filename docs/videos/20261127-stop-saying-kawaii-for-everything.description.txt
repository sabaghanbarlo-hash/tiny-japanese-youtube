Stop Saying “Kawaii” for Everything! Say These | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

かわいい！ かわいい！ かわいい！ - Cute! Cute! Cute!
ぜんぶ かわいい？ - Everything is cute?
ふくは「すてき」。 - For clothes: “suteki” (lovely).
くつは「かっこいい」。 - For shoes: “kakkoii” (cool).
すてき！ かっこいい！ - Lovely! Cool!

WORDS
すてき (Suteki) - lovely / stylish
かっこいい (Kakkoii) - cool
