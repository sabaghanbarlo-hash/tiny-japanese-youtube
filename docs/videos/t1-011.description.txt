VOCABULARY
あね (Ane) - older sister (one's own) (姉)
いもうと (Imouto) - younger sister (妹)
あに (Ani) - older brother (one's own) (兄)
おとうと (Otouto) - younger brother (弟)
おじいさん (Ojiisan) - grandfather
おばあさん (Obaasan) - grandmother
おっと (Otto) - husband (夫)
つま (Tsuma) - wife (妻)
りょうしん (Ryoushin) - parents (両親)
しんせき (Shinseki) - relative (親戚)

GRAMMAR
Using も for “also” - も comes after a noun to mean “also” or “too.” It adds another person or thing to what was already mentioned.
