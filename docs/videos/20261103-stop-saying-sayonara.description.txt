Stop Saying “Sayonara” — Say This Instead | Learn Japanese in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny Japanese story and a quick quiz with Mika and Alex. Follow for a new lesson every day!

みんな、さようなら！ - Goodbye, everyone!
えっ、さようならは ちょっと おおげさだよ。 - Huh, sayonara is a bit dramatic.
ほんとう？ - Really?
ふつうは「またね」か「じゃあね」。 - Usually it's “mata ne” or “jaa ne.”
またね！ - See you!
またね！ - See you!

WORDS
またね (Matane) - see you (later)
じゃあね (Jaane) - bye (casual)
