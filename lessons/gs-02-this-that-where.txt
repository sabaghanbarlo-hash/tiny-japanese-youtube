[grammar]
grammar: The こそあど system | こ = close to the speaker, そ = close to the listener, あ = far from both, ど = question. これ, それ, あれ, どれ are things. ここ, そこ, あそこ, どこ are places.
example: すみません、これは なん ですか。 | Excuse me, what is this?
example: それは けしゴム です。 | That is an eraser.
grammar: これ stands alone, この needs a noun | Use これ by itself: これは ほん です。 Use この with a noun right after it: この ほんは わたしの です。 The same goes for その and あの.
example: すみません、これは なん ですか。 | Excuse me, what is this?

[quiz]
quiz: Which word means “that over there” (far from both)? | あれ | これ | それ | A | あ words are far from both speaker and listener.
quiz: Which word means “here”? | そこ | ここ | あそこ | B | ここ is the こ (near me) place word.
quiz: What does どこ mean? | who | what | where | C | どこ asks about a place.
