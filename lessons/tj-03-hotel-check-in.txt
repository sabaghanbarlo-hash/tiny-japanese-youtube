[grammar]
grammar: から and まで for ranges | から means “from” and まで means “until”: しちじから くじまで (from seven to nine). Use them for opening hours, breakfast times and check-out times.
example: あさごはんは なんじから ですか。 | From what time is breakfast?
example: しちじから くじまで です。 | From seven to nine.
grammar: Counting nights and floors | Add はく for nights: いっぱく (1), にはく (2), さんぱく (3). Add かい for floors: いっかい (1st), にかい (2nd), ごかい (5th). Some counters change their sound, so learn them as they come.
example: こんにちは。チェックインを おねがいします。 | Hello. Check-in, please.
example: おなまえを おねがいします。 | Your name, please.

quiz: What does しちじから くじまで mean? | at seven or nine | seven times nine | from seven to nine o'clock | C | から = from, まで = until.
