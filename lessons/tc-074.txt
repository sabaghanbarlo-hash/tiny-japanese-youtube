title: Tiny Conversations · Lesson 74 · Where Does It Hurt?
episode: TC-074
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: こんにちは。私は 看護師です。 | Konnichiwa. Watashi wa kangoshi desu. | Hello. I’m a nurse.
Alex: こんにちは。アレックスです。 | Konnichiwa. Arekkusu desu. | Hello. I’m Alex.
Mika: 今日は どうしましたか。 | Kyou wa dou shimashita ka. | What brings you in today?
Alex: 頭が 痛いです。 | Atama ga itai desu. | My head hurts.
Mika: いつから 痛いですか。 | Itsu kara itai desu ka. | Since when has it hurt?
Alex: 昨日からです。 | Kinou kara desu. | Since yesterday.
Mika: 熱は ありますか。 | Netsu wa arimasu ka. | Do you have a fever?
Alex: いいえ、熱は ありません。 | Iie, netsu wa arimasen. | No, I don’t have a fever.
Mika: 咳も ありますか。 | Seki mo arimasu ka. | Do you also have a cough?
Alex: いいえ。でも、喉も 痛いです。 | Iie. Demo, nodo mo itai desu. | No. But my throat hurts too.
Mika: そうですか。少し 休んでください。 | Sou desu ka. Sukoshi yasunde kudasai. | I see. Please rest a little.
Alex: はい。ありがとう。 | Hai. Arigatou. | Okay. Thank you.

[grammar]
grammar: Say what hurts | Use [body part] が 痛いです to say that a body part hurts. Example: 頭が 痛いです。

[quiz]
quiz: What hurts Alex? | His head and throat | His head only | His throat only | A | Alex says his head hurts, then adds that his throat hurts too.
quiz: Since when has Alex’s head hurt? | Today | Yesterday | Tomorrow | B | Alex says 昨日からです, meaning “since yesterday.”
quiz: Does Alex have a fever? | Yes | No | The dialogue doesn’t say | B | Alex answers 熱は ありません, meaning he doesn’t have a fever.
