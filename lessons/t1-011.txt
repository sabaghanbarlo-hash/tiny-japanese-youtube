title: Tiny 1000 · Lesson 11 · Family and relatives
episode: T1-011
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: ご家族 は 何人 ですか。 | Gokazoku wa nan-nin desu ka? | How many people are in your family?
Alex: 両親 と 姉 と 兄 と 弟 が います。 | Ryōshin to ane to ani to otōto ga imasu. | I have parents, an older sister, an older brother, and a younger brother.
Mika: おじいさん と おばあさん も いますか。 | Ojiisan to obaasan mo imasu ka? | Do you also have a grandfather and grandmother?
Alex: はい、います。妹 も います。 | Hai, imasu. Imōto mo imasu. | Yes, I do. I also have a younger sister.
Mika: 親戚 は 近く に 住んでいますか。 | Shinseki wa chikaku ni sunde imasu ka? | Do your relatives live nearby?
Alex: はい、近く に います。姉 の 夫 は やさしいです。 | Hai, chikaku ni imasu. Ane no otto wa yasashii desu. | Yes, they live nearby. My older sister’s husband is kind.
Mika: 兄 の 妻 も やさしいですか。 | Ani no tsuma mo yasashii desu ka? | Is your older brother’s wife kind too?
Alex: はい。妹 と 弟 も なかよしです。 | Hai. Imōto to otōto mo nakayoshi desu. | Yes. My younger sister and younger brother are good friends too.

[vocab]
vocab: あね | older sister (one's own) (姉)
example: 姉は優しいです。 | My older sister is kind.
vocab: いもうと | younger sister (妹)
example: 妹は高校生です。 | My younger sister is a high school student.
vocab: あに | older brother (one's own) (兄)
example: 兄は大学生です。 | My older brother is a university student.
vocab: おとうと | younger brother (弟)
example: 弟は元気です。 | My younger brother is well.
vocab: おじいさん | grandfather
example: おじいさんは元気です。 | My grandfather is well.
vocab: おばあさん | grandmother
example: おばあさんは料理が上手です。 | My grandmother is good at cooking.
vocab: おっと | husband (夫)
example: 夫は優しいです。 | My husband is kind.
vocab: つま | wife (妻)
example: 妻は絵が上手です。 | My wife is good at drawing.
vocab: りょうしん | parents (両親)
example: 両親は元気です。 | My parents are well.
vocab: しんせき | relative (親戚)
example: 親戚は親切です。 | My relative is kind.

[grammar]
grammar: Using も for “also” | も comes after a noun to mean “also” or “too.” It adds another person or thing to what was already mentioned.
example: おじいさん と おばあさん も いますか。 | Do you also have a grandfather and grandmother?
example: 妹 も います。 | I also have a younger sister.
