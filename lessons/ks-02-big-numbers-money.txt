[grammar]
grammar: Sound changes in 百 and 千 | 百 is ひゃく, but 300 is さんびゃく, 600 is ろっぴゃく and 800 is はっぴゃく. 千 is せん, but 3,000 is さんぜん and 8,000 is はっせん. These changes make the words easier to say.
example: 三百円 です。 | It's 300 yen.
example: 千二百円 です。 | It's 1,200 yen.
grammar: 一 before 万 | 10,000 is 一万 (いちまん). Unlike 百 and 千, you must say いち before 万. One yen at the till is 一円 (いちえん).
example: すみません、これは いくら ですか。 | Excuse me, how much is this?
example: 三百円 です。 | It's 300 yen.
