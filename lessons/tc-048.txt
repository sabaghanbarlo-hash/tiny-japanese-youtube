title: Tiny Conversations · Lesson 48 · A Delayed Train
episode: TC-048
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: すみません、この 電車は まだ 来ませんか。 | Sumimasen, kono densha wa mada kimasen ka? | Excuse me, is this train still not coming?
Alex: はい、十分 くらい 遅れて います。 | Hai, juppun kurai okurete imasu. | Yes, it’s about ten minutes late.
Mika: そうですか。理由は 何ですか。 | Sou desu ka. Riyuu wa nan desu ka? | I see. What’s the reason?
Alex: 事故が 理由かもしれません。 | Jiko ga riyuu kamo shiremasen. | An accident might be the reason.
Mika: そうですか。ここで 待ちます。 | Sou desu ka. Koko de machimasu. | I see. I’ll wait here.
Alex: 私も 待ちます。 | Watashi mo machimasu. | I’ll wait too.
Mika: 電車は いつ 来ますか。 | Densha wa itsu kimasu ka? | When will the train come?
Alex: もうすぐ 来ると 思います。 | Mou sugu kuru to omoimasu. | I think it will come soon.
Mika: ありがとうございます。少し 安心しました。 | Arigatou gozaimasu. Sukoshi anshin shimashita. | Thank you. I feel a little relieved.
Alex: どういたしまして。駅は 寒いですね。 | Dou itashimashite. Eki wa samui desu ne. | You’re welcome. The station is cold, isn’t it?

[grammar]
grammar: まだ〜ませんか | Use this to ask whether something has not happened yet. Example: 「この 電車は まだ 来ませんか。」

[quiz]
quiz: About how late is the train? | About ten minutes | About two minutes | About one hour | A | Alex says it is delayed by about ten minutes.
quiz: What might be the reason for the delay? | An accident | Rain | A broken phone | A | Alex says an accident might be the reason.
quiz: Where are Mika and Alex waiting? | At the station | At home | At school | A | They talk about waiting there and say the station is cold.
