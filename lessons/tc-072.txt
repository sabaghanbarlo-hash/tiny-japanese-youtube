title: Tiny Conversations · Lesson 72 · Making a Clinic Appointment
episode: TC-072
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: こんにちは。病院の予約をお願いします。 | Konnichiwa. Byouin no yoyaku o onegai shimasu. | Hello. I’d like to make an appointment at the hospital.
Alex: はい。病院の受付です。いつがよろしいですか。 | Hai. Byouin no uketsuke desu. Itsu ga yoroshii desu ka. | Yes. This is the hospital reception desk. When works for you?
Mika: 明日の午前十時は大丈夫ですか。 | Ashita no gozen juuji wa daijoubu desu ka. | Would tomorrow at 10 a.m. be okay?
Alex: はい。明日の午前十時に予約できます。 | Hai. Ashita no gozen juuji ni yoyaku dekimasu. | Yes. You can make an appointment for tomorrow at 10 a.m.
Mika: 風邪です。熱もあります。 | Kaze desu. Netsu mo arimasu. | I have a cold and a fever.
Alex: そうですか。名前をお願いします。 | Sou desu ka. Namae o onegai shimasu. | I see. May I have your name?
Mika: ミカです。 | Mika desu. | I’m Mika.
Alex: 予約は明日の午前十時です。 | Yoyaku wa ashita no gozen juuji desu. | Your appointment is tomorrow at 10 a.m.
Mika: ありがとうございます。明日、病院に行きます。 | Arigatou gozaimasu. Ashita, byouin ni ikimasu. | Thank you. I’ll go to the hospital tomorrow.
Alex: 受付で待ってください。 | Uketsuke de matte kudasai. | Please wait at the reception desk.
Mika: はい。ありがとうございます。 | Hai. Arigatou gozaimasu. | Yes. Thank you.
Alex: お大事に。失礼します。 | Odaiji ni. Shitsurei shimasu. | Take care. Goodbye.

[grammar]
grammar: 〜をお願いします | A polite way to ask for something. Example: 予約をお願いします。

[quiz]
quiz: When is Mika’s appointment? | Tomorrow at 10 a.m. | Today at 10 a.m. | Tomorrow in the evening | A | They confirm 明日の午前十時, meaning tomorrow at 10 a.m.
quiz: What does Mika say she has? | A headache and a cough | A cold and a fever | An injury | B | Mika says 風邪です。熱もあります, a cold and a fever.
quiz: Where should Mika wait? | At the hospital reception desk | At the station | At home | A | Alex says 受付で待ってください, meaning to wait at reception.
