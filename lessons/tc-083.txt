title: Tiny Conversations · Lesson 83 · Joining a Music Club
episode: TC-083
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: こんにちは。音楽の チームに 入りたいです。 | Konnichiwa. Ongaku no chiimu ni hairitai desu. | Hello. I want to join the music team.
Alex: こんにちは。もちろんです。 | Konnichiwa. Mochiron desu. | Hello. Of course.
Mika: いつ 練習しますか。 | Itsu renshuu shimasu ka. | When do you practice?
Alex: 土曜日の 午後です。 | Doyoubi no gogo desu. | Saturday afternoon.
Mika: どこで 練習しますか。 | Doko de renshuu shimasu ka. | Where do you practice?
Alex: 学校で 練習します。 | Gakkou de renshuu shimasu. | We practice at school.
Mika: 何も 持っていません。 | Nani mo motte imasen. | I don't have anything.
Alex: 大丈夫です。歌えます。 | Daijoubu desu. Utaemasu. | That's okay. You can sing.
Mika: 歌うのが 好きです。 | Utau no ga suki desu. | I like singing.
Alex: いいですね。一緒に 練習しましょう。 | Ii desu ne. Issho ni renshuu shimashou. | That sounds good. Let's practice together.
Mika: 参加するには どうしますか。 | Sanka suru ni wa dou shimasu ka. | What do I do to join?
Alex: ここで 申し込みます。 | Koko de moushikomimasu. | You sign up here.

[grammar]
grammar: Saying you want to do something | Add たいです to a verb stem to say you want to do something. Example: 入りたいです means “I want to join.”

[quiz]
quiz: When does the group practice? | Saturday afternoon | Sunday morning | Monday evening | A | Alex says 土曜日の 午後です, meaning Saturday afternoon.
quiz: Where does the group practice? | At home | At school | At the station | B | Alex says 学校で 練習します, meaning they practice at school.
quiz: What does Mika say she likes? | Playing sports | Singing | Reading | B | Mika says 歌うのが 好きです, meaning she likes singing.
