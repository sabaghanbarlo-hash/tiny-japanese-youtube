title: Tiny Conversations · Lesson 53 · Meet My Family
episode: TC-053
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: みんな、私の友達のアレックスです。 | Minna, watashi no tomodachi no Arekkusu desu. | Everyone, this is my friend Alex.
Alex: 初めまして。アレックスです。よろしくお願いします。 | Hajimemashite. Arekkusu desu. Yoroshiku onegaishimasu. | Nice to meet you. I’m Alex. It’s nice to meet you.
Mika: 母です。 | Haha desu. | This is my mother.
Alex: 初めまして。会えて 嬉しいです。 | Hajimemashite. Aete ureshii desu. | Nice to meet you. I’m happy to meet you.
Mika: 父です。 | Chichi desu. | This is my father.
Alex: こんにちは。よろしくお願いします。 | Konnichiwa. Yoroshiku onegaishimasu. | Hello. It’s nice to meet you.
Mika: 妹です。 | Imouto desu. | This is my younger sister.
Alex: 初めまして。どうぞ よろしくお願いします。 | Hajimemashite. Douzo yoroshiku onegaishimasu. | Nice to meet you. It’s a pleasure to meet you.
Mika: お茶を 飲みますか。 | Ocha o nomimasu ka. | Would you like some tea?
Alex: はい、ありがとう。お茶は おいしいです。 | Hai, arigatou. Ocha wa oishii desu. | Yes, thank you. The tea is delicious.

[grammar]
grammar: Introducing a friend | Use 「A の B です」 to identify someone as a friend. Example: 私の友達のアレックスです。

[quiz]
quiz: Who is Alex introduced as? | Mika’s friend | Mika’s father | Mika’s younger sister | A | Mika says 私の友達のアレックスです, meaning Alex is her friend.
quiz: Which family member does Mika introduce after her mother? | Her younger sister | Her father | Her friend | B | Mika says 父です after introducing her mother.
quiz: What does Alex say about the tea? | It is delicious. | It is cold. | It is expensive. | A | Alex says お茶は おいしいです, meaning the tea is delicious.
