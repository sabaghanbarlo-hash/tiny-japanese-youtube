title: Tiny 1000 · Lesson 60 · Quantity and amount words
episode: T1-060
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: りんごは いくつ 買いますか。 | Ringo wa ikutsu kaimasu ka. | How many apples will you buy?
Alex: りんごを 全部で 六つ 買います。 | Ringo o zenbu de muttsu kaimasu. | I’ll buy six apples in all.
Mika: 六つは 多いですか、少ないですか。 | Muttsu wa ooi desu ka, sukunai desu ka. | Is six a lot, or a little?
Alex: ちょうど いいです。半分は 家族に あげます。 | Choudo ii desu. Hanbun wa kazoku ni agemasu. | That’s just right. I’ll give half to my family.
Mika: お金は いくら ありますか。 | Okane wa ikura arimasu ka. | How much money do you have?
Alex: １０００円 あります。 | Sen-en arimasu. | I have 1,000 yen.
Mika: それで お金は 足りますか。 | Sore de okane wa tarimasu ka. | Will that be enough money?
Alex: はい。少し お金が 余ります。 | Hai. Sukoshi okane ga amarimasu. | Yes. I’ll have a little money left over.

[vocab]
vocab: おおい | many; a lot (多い)
example: 人が多いです。 | There are many people.
vocab: すくない | few; little (少ない)
example: 今日は人が少ないです。 | There are few people today.
vocab: ぜんぶ | all; everything (全部)
example: これで全部です。 | This is everything.
vocab: はんぶん | half (半分)
example: ケーキを半分食べました。 | I ate half the cake.
vocab: いくつ | how many
example: りんごはいくつありますか。 | How many apples are there?
vocab: いくら | how much
example: この服はいくらですか。 | How much is this item of clothing?
vocab: たりる | to be enough (足りる)
example: 水はこれで足ります。 | This water is enough.
vocab: あまる | to be left over (余る)
example: 時間が余りました。 | We had time left over.
vocab: ちょうど | exactly; just
example: ちょうど三人います。 | There are exactly three people.
vocab: いっぱい | a lot; full
example: 水をいっぱい飲みました。 | I drank a lot of water.

[grammar]
grammar: Asking About Quantity | Use いくつ to ask how many items someone will buy. Use いくら to ask how much money someone has.
example: りんごは いくつ 買いますか。 | How many apples will you buy?
example: お金は いくら ありますか。 | How much money do you have?

[quiz]
quiz: What does いくつ mean? | a lot | few | how many | C | いくつ means how many.
quiz: What does いっぱい mean? | a lot | how many | how much | A | いっぱい means a lot.
quiz: What does 全部 mean? | how many | few | all | C | 全部 (ぜんぶ) means all.
quiz: Review - What does 奥 mean? | near | side | back | C | 奥 (おく) means back.
quiz: Review - What does 運ぶ mean? | turn on | close | carry | C | 運ぶ (はこぶ) means carry.
quiz: Review - What does サッカー mean? | baseball | match | soccer | C | サッカー means soccer.
