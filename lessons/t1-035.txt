title: Tiny 1000 · Lesson 35 · Frequency and degree adverbs
episode: T1-035
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: アレックスさん は いつも 日本語 を 勉強しますか。 | Arekkusu-san wa itsumo Nihongo o benkyou shimasu ka. | Alex, do you always study Japanese?
Alex: はい、毎日 よく 勉強します。 | Hai, mainichi yoku benkyou shimasu. | Yes, I study often every day.
Mika: 日本語 の 音楽 は 時々 聞きますか。 | Nihongo no ongaku wa tokidoki kikimasu ka. | Do you sometimes listen to Japanese music?
Alex: はい、たまに 聞きます。歌 は すごく 楽しいです。 | Hai, tama ni kikimasu. Uta wa sugoku tanoshii desu. | Yes, once in a while. The songs are really fun.
Mika: 日本 の ドラマ は どうですか。 | Nihon no dorama wa dou desu ka. | How are Japanese dramas?
Alex: かなり 好きです。でも、字幕 なし では ほとんど 分かりません。 | Kanari suki desu. Demo, jimaku nashi de wa hotondo wakarimasen. | I like them quite a lot. But I understand almost nothing without subtitles.
Mika: そうですか。漢字 は あまり 読みませんか。 | Sou desu ka. Kanji wa amari yomimasen ka. | I see. Do you not read kanji very much?
Alex: はい、全然 読めません。ちょっと 難しいです。 | Hai, zenzen yomemasen. Chotto muzukashii desu. | Yes, I can't read them at all. They're a little difficult.

[vocab]
vocab: いつも | always
example: いつも早く寝ます。 | I always go to bed early.
vocab: よく | often
example: よく散歩します。 | I often take walks.
vocab: ときどき | sometimes (時々)
example: 時々雨が降ります。 | It sometimes rains.
vocab: たまに | once in a while
example: たまに外食します。 | I eat out once in a while.
vocab: あまり | not very; not much
example: あまりテレビを見ません。 | I don't watch much TV.
vocab: ぜんぜん | not at all (全然)
example: 全然眠くないです。 | I'm not sleepy at all.
vocab: かなり | quite; considerably
example: 今日はかなり寒いです。 | It's quite cold today.
vocab: ちょっと | a little; a bit
example: ちょっと休みます。 | I'll rest a little.
vocab: すごく | very; really
example: すごく元気です。 | I'm feeling really good.
vocab: ほとんど | almost; mostly
example: 宿題はほとんど終わりました。 | The homework is almost done.

[grammar]
grammar: Adverbs Before Verbs | Frequency adverbs usually come before the verb they describe. Degree adverbs usually come before the adjective they describe.
example: はい、毎日 よく 勉強します。 | Yes, I study often every day.
example: はい、たまに 聞きます。歌 は すごく 楽しいです。 | Yes, once in a while. The songs are really fun.

[quiz]
quiz: What does すごく mean? | almost | often | very | C | すごく means very.
quiz: What does 全然 mean? | not at all | once in a while | almost | A | 全然 (ぜんぜん) means not at all.
quiz: What does 時々 mean? | sometimes | not at all | not very | A | 時々 (ときどき) means sometimes.
quiz: Review - What does も mean? | with | about | also | C | も means also.
quiz: Review - What does 決める mean? | to differ | opinion | to decide | C | 決める (きめる) means to decide.
quiz: Review - What does 借りる mean? | receive | borrow | can do | B | 借りる (かりる) means borrow.
