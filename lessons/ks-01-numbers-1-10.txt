[grammar]
grammar: Two readings for 4, 7 and 9 | 四 is よん or し, 七 is なな or しち, 九 is きゅう or く. Counting uses よん, なな and きゅう. But 4 o'clock, 7 o'clock and 9 o'clock use the older readings: よじ, しちじ, くじ.
example: なん人 ですか。 | How many people?
example: 三人 です。ミカさんと わたしと ともだち です。 | Three. You, me and a friend.
grammar: Numbers combine by position | 十 is ten, so 二十 is two tens (20), 三十 is thirty and 十一 is eleven (ten + one). The kanji add up like arithmetic: 十二 = 12.
example: なん人 ですか。 | How many people?
