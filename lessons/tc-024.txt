title: Tiny Conversations · Lesson 24 · No Meat, Please
episode: TC-024
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: すみません、肉は 食べません。 | Sumimasen, niku wa tabemasen. | Excuse me, I don’t eat meat.
Alex: そうですか。 | Sou desu ka. | I see.
Mika: 肉のない料理は ありますか。 | Niku no nai ryouri wa arimasu ka. | Do you have a dish without meat?
Alex: はい、野菜と豆腐の料理が あります。 | Hai, yasai to toufu no ryouri ga arimasu. | Yes, we have a vegetable and tofu dish.
Mika: 卵も 入っていますか。 | Tamago mo haitte imasu ka. | Does it have egg in it too?
Alex: いいえ、卵は 入っていません。 | Iie, tamago wa haitte imasen. | No, it doesn’t have egg in it.
Mika: それは おいしいですか。 | Sore wa oishii desu ka. | Is it tasty?
Alex: はい、とても おいしいです。 | Hai, totemo oishii desu. | Yes, it’s very tasty.
Mika: じゃあ、それを ください。 | Jaa, sore o kudasai. | Then, I’ll have that, please.
Alex: はい、どうぞ。 | Hai, douzo. | Certainly. Here you are.

[grammar]
grammar: Asking if something is available | Use 「〜は ありますか」 to ask whether something is available. Example: 「肉のない料理は ありますか。」

[quiz]
quiz: What kind of dish does Alex say is available? | A vegetable and tofu dish | A fish dish | A meat dish | A | Alex says there is a dish with vegetables and tofu.
quiz: Does the dish contain egg? | Yes | No | The dialogue doesn’t say | B | Alex says egg is not in the dish.
quiz: What does Mika order? | Water | The vegetable and tofu dish | A menu | B | Mika says 「それを ください」, ordering the dish they discussed.
