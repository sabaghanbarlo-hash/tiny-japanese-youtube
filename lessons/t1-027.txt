title: Tiny 1000 · Lesson 27 · Verbs of movement
episode: T1-027
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: アレックスさん、駅まで どうやって 向かいますか。 | Arekkusu-san, eki made dou yatte mukaimasu ka. | Alex, how will you get to the station?
Alex: まず 公園まで 歩きます。 | Mazu kouen made arukimasu. | First, I walk to the park.
Mika: 橋を 渡って、右に 曲がりますか。 | Hashi o watatte, migi ni magarimasu ka. | Do you cross the bridge and turn right?
Alex: はい。それから まっすぐ 進みます。 | Hai. Sorekara massugu susumimasu. | Yes. Then I go straight ahead.
Mika: どの道を 通りますか。 | Dono michi o toorimasu ka. | Which road do you take?
Alex: 小さい店に 寄って、駅に 着きます。 | Chiisai mise ni yotte, eki ni tsukimasu. | I stop by a small shop and arrive at the station.
Mika: 駅に 近づいたら、走りますか。 | Eki ni chikazuitara, hashirimasu ka. | When you get close to the station, do you run?
Alex: いいえ、走りません。ゆっくり 歩きます。 | Iie, hashirimasen. Yukkuri arukimasu. | No, I don't run. I walk slowly.

[vocab]
vocab: あるく | to walk (歩く)
example: 公園を歩きます。 | I walk in the park.
vocab: はしる | to run (走る)
example: 朝、走ります。 | I run in the morning.
vocab: つく | to arrive (着く)
example: 駅に着きます。 | I arrive at the station.
vocab: まがる | to turn (曲がる)
example: 次の角を右に曲がります。 | Turn right at the next corner.
vocab: わたる | to cross (渡る)
example: 橋を渡ります。 | I cross the bridge.
vocab: すすむ | to move forward; to proceed (進む)
example: 公園までまっすぐ進みます。 | I go straight ahead to the park.
vocab: とおる | to pass through; to go along (通る)
example: 毎朝、この道を通ります。 | I go along this road every morning.
vocab: むかう | to head toward (向かう)
example: 駅に向かいます。 | I’m heading to the station.
vocab: よる | to stop by (寄る)
example: 仕事の後、店に寄ります。 | I’ll stop by the store after work.
vocab: ちかづく | to approach; to get closer (近づく)
example: バスが近づいています。 | The bus is coming closer.

[grammar]
grammar: Connecting Actions with て | The て-form connects actions in order. It often means “and then” in simple directions.
example: 橋を 渡って、右に 曲がりますか。 | Do you cross the bridge and turn right?
example: 小さい店に 寄って、駅に 着きます。 | I stop by a small shop and arrive at the station.

[quiz]
quiz: What does 向かう mean? | to head toward | to pass through | to approach | A | 向かう (むかう) means to head toward.
quiz: What does 渡る mean? | to cross | to head toward | to run | A | 渡る (わたる) means to cross.
quiz: What does 寄る mean? | to run | to cross | to stop by | C | 寄る (よる) means to stop by.
quiz: Review - What does 花 mean? | sky | lake | flower | C | 花 (はな) means flower.
quiz: Review - What does 空港 mean? | bicycle | get off | airport | C | 空港 (くうこう) means airport.
quiz: Review - What does 冷たい mean? | cold (to the touch) | shallow | far | A | 冷たい (つめたい) means cold (to the touch).
