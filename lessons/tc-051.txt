title: Tiny Conversations · Lesson 51 · Welcome Home
episode: TC-051
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: アレックス、こんばんは。どうぞ。 | Arekkusu, konbanwa. Douzo. | Good evening, Alex. Come in.
Alex: こんばんは。ありがとう。 | Konbanwa. Arigatou. | Good evening. Thank you.
Mika: ここは 私の 家です。 | Koko wa watashi no ie desu. | This is my home.
Alex: 大きい 家ですね。 | Ookii ie desu ne. | Your house is big.
Mika: どうぞ 入って ください。 | Douzo haitte kudasai. | Please come in.
Alex: はい。きれいな 家ですね。 | Hai. Kirei na ie desu ne. | Yes. Your house is beautiful.
Mika: ありがとう。部屋は ここです。 | Arigatou. Heya wa koko desu. | Thank you. The room is here.
Alex: 部屋も とても きれいですね。 | Heya mo totemo kirei desu ne. | The room is very nice too.
Mika: お茶を 飲みますか。 | Ocha o nomimasu ka. | Would you like some tea?
Alex: はい、いただきます。 | Hai, itadakimasu. | Yes, thank you.
Mika: お茶を どうぞ。 | Ocha o douzo. | Here’s some tea.
Alex: ありがとう。おいしいです。 | Arigatou. Oishii desu. | Thank you. It’s delicious.

[grammar]
grammar: どうぞ | Use どうぞ to politely invite someone in or offer something. Example: どうぞ 入って ください。

[quiz]
quiz: What does Mika offer Alex? | Tea | Coffee | Water | A | Mika asks if Alex would like tea and offers お茶.
quiz: Where does Mika say the room is? | Here | There | Outside | A | Mika says 部屋は ここです, meaning the room is here.
quiz: What does Alex think of the tea? | It is delicious | It is cold | It is expensive | A | Alex says おいしいです, meaning it is delicious.
