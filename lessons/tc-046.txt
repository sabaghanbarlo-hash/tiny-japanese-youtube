title: Tiny Conversations · Lesson 46 · Finding a Taxi
episode: TC-046
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: すみません、タクシーを 呼んで ください。 | Sumimasen, takushii o yonde kudasai. | Excuse me, please call a taxi.
Alex: はい、どこまで 行きますか。 | Hai, doko made ikimasu ka. | Certainly. Where are you going?
Mika: 駅まで お願いします。 | Eki made onegaishimasu. | To the station, please.
Alex: はい、今 タクシーを 呼びます。 | Hai, ima takushii o yobimasu. | Certainly. I’ll call a taxi now.
Mika: どこで 待ちますか。 | Doko de machimasu ka. | Where should I wait?
Alex: ここで 待って ください。 | Koko de matte kudasai. | Please wait here.
Mika: はい、ここで 待ちます。 | Hai, koko de machimasu. | Okay, I’ll wait here.
Alex: タクシーは もうすぐ 来ます。 | Takushii wa mousugu kimasu. | The taxi will be here soon.
Mika: ありがとう。 | Arigatou. | Thank you.
Alex: どういたしまして。 | Dou itashimashite. | You’re welcome.

[grammar]
grammar: Destination + まで お願いします | Use this to tell someone your destination politely. Example: 駅まで お願いします。

[quiz]
quiz: Where does Mika want to go? | The station | The hotel | The school | A | Mika says 駅まで, meaning “to the station.”
quiz: Where does Alex ask Mika to wait? | There | Here | At the station | B | Alex says ここで 待って ください, meaning “Please wait here.”
quiz: When will the taxi arrive? | Soon | Tomorrow | Alex doesn’t say | A | Alex says もうすぐ 来ます, meaning “It will come soon.”
