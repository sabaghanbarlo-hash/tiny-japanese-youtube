[grammar]
grammar: The pattern | Each day is one kanji + 曜日 (ようび). 日 (nichi) Sunday, 月 (getsu) Monday, 火 (ka) Tuesday, 水 (sui) Wednesday, 木 (moku) Thursday, 金 (kin) Friday, 土 (do) Saturday. The on readings are the ones you hear.
example: 今日は 何曜日 ですか。 | What day of the week is it today?
example: 火曜日 です。 | It's Tuesday.
grammar: Kanji with more than one job | 日 also means “day” and “sun” (ひ), 月 means “moon” and “month” (つき, がつ), 水 is “water” (みず), 金 is “gold” or “money” (かね), 木 is “tree” (き), 土 is “earth” (つち). So 一月 is January, and お金 is money.
example: 今日は 何曜日 ですか。 | What day of the week is it today?
example: 火曜日 です。 | It's Tuesday.
