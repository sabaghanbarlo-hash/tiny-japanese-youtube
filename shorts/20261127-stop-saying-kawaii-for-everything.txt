title: Stop Saying “Kawaii” for Everything! Say These
scene: store

[story]
Alex: かわいい！ かわいい！ かわいい！ | Cute! Cute! Cute!
Mika: ぜんぶ かわいい？ | Everything is cute?
Mika: ふくは「すてき」。 | For clothes: “suteki” (lovely).
Mika: くつは「かっこいい」。 | For shoes: “kakkoii” (cool).
Alex: すてき！ かっこいい！ | Lovely! Cool!

[vocab]
vocab: すてき | lovely / stylish
example: すてきな ぼうしですね。 | What a lovely hat!
vocab: かっこいい | cool
example: かっこいい くつですね。 | Those are cool shoes!

[quiz]
quiz: Which one means “cool”? | かっこいい | おいしい | すてき | A | かっこいい means cool or stylish.
