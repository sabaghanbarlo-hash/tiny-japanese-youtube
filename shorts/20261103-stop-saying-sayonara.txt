title: Stop Saying “Sayonara” — Say This Instead
scene: station

[story]
Alex: みんな、さようなら！ | Goodbye, everyone!
Mika: えっ、さようならは ちょっと おおげさだよ。 | Huh, sayonara is a bit dramatic.
Alex: ほんとう？ | Really?
Mika: ふつうは「またね」か「じゃあね」。 | Usually it's “mata ne” or “jaa ne.”
Alex: またね！ | See you!
Mika: またね！ | See you!

[vocab]
vocab: またね | see you (later)
example: あした、またね！ | See you tomorrow!
vocab: じゃあね | bye (casual)
example: じゃあね、ミカ！ | Bye, Mika!

[quiz]
quiz: What do friends say when they'll meet again soon? | さようなら | またね | はじめまして | B | またね means “see you.”
