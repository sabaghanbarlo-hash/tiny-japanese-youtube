title: 3 Ways to Say “Yes” in Japanese
scene: classroom

[story]
Mika: にほんごが すき？ | Do you like Japanese?
Alex: はい！ | Yes!
Mika: ていねいに いうと「はい」、やわらかく「ええ」。 | Polite: “hai.” Softer: “ee.”
Mika: ともだちには「うん」。 | With friends: “un.”
Alex: うん！ | Yeah!

[vocab]
vocab: ええ | yes (soft, polite)
example: ええ、そうです。 | Yes, that's right.
vocab: うん | yeah (casual)
example: うん、いいよ！ | Yeah, sure!

[quiz]
quiz: Which one is casual? | はい | ええ | うん | C | うん is casual, for friends.
