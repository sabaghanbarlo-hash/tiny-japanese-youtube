title: 3 Tiny Japanese Words
episode: Short 5
scene: colorful vocabulary cards

[hook]

Mika: きょうは、みじかい にほんごを みっつ！ | Kyou wa, mijikai nihongo o mittsu! | Today, three short Japanese expressions!

[story]

Alex: はい！ | Hai! | Yes!

Mika: いいえ！ | Iie! | No!

Alex: ありがとう！ | Arigatou! | Thank you!

[practice]

Mika: 「はい」は yes！ | “Hai” wa yes! | “Hai” means yes!

Alex: 「いいえ」は no！ | “Iie” wa no! | “Iie” means no!

Mika: 「ありがとう」は thank you！ | “Arigatou” wa thank you! | “Arigatou” means thank you!

Alex: じゃあ、クイズ！「Thank you」は どれ？ | Jaa, kuizu! “Thank you” wa dore? | Now, a quiz! Which one means “thank you”?

Mika: A、はい！ B、いいえ！ C、ありがとう！ | A, hai! B, iie! C, arigatou! | A, yes! B, no! C, thank you!

Alex: こたえは C！ありがとう！ | Kotae wa C! Arigatou! | The answer is C! Arigatou!

[ending]

Mika and Alex: また らいしゅう！ | Mata raishuu! | See you next week!
