title: How to Ask “How Much Is This?” in Japanese
scene: store

[story]
Mika: いらっしゃいませ！ | Welcome!
Alex: すみません、これは いくらですか。 | Excuse me, how much is this?
Mika: それは ごひゃくえんです。 | That's 500 yen.
Alex: やすい！ これを おねがいします。 | Cheap! This one, please.
Mika: ありがとう ございます！ | Thank you very much!

[vocab]
vocab: いくら | how much
example: これは いくらですか。 | How much is this?
vocab: ごひゃくえん | 500 yen
example: ごひゃくえんです。 | It's 500 yen.

[quiz]
quiz: What does これは いくらですか mean? | How much is this? | Where is this? | Who is this? | A | いくら means “how much.”
