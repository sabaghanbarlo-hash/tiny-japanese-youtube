title: Make Plans in Japanese
episode: Short 4
scene: school entrance

[hook]

Alex: ともだちを あそびに さそいたい！ | Tomodachi o asobi ni sasoitai! | You want to invite a friend to hang out!

Mika: そんなときは、こう いってみて！ | Sonna toki wa, kou itte mite! | In that case, try saying this!

[story]

Alex: きょう、ひま？ | Kyou, hima? | Are you free today?

Mika: うん、ひまだよ！ | Un, hima da yo! | Yes, I'm free!

Alex: いっしょに こうえんに いかない？ | Issho ni kouen ni ikanai? | Would you like to go to the park together?

Mika: いいね！いこう！ | Ii ne! Ikou! | Sounds good! Let's go!

[learning tip]

Mika: 「ひま？」は「Are you free?」という いみだよ。 | “Hima?” wa “Are you free?” to iu imi da yo. | “Hima?” means “Are you free?”

Alex: 「いかない？」は、カジュアルな さそいかただよ。 | “Ikanai?” wa, kajuaru na sasoikata da yo. | “Ikanai?” is a casual way to invite someone.

[ending]

Mika: ともだちに つかってみてね！ | Tomodachi ni tsukatte mite ne! | Try using it with a friend!
