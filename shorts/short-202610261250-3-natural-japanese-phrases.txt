title: 3 Natural Japanese Phrases
scene: park

[story]
Mika: アレックス、サッカーが すきなの？ | Alex, do you like soccer?
Alex: うん、だいすき！ | Yes, I love it!
Mika: そうなんだ！ | Oh, I see!
Alex: ミカは えを かくのが すき？ | Do you like drawing, Mika?
Mika: うん、すきだよ！ | Yes, I do!
Alex: いいね！ | That's nice!
Mika: ほんとう？ | Really?
Alex: うん、ほんとう！ | Yes, really!

[vocab]
vocab: そうなんだ | Oh, I see / Really
example: そうなんだ！おもしろいね。 | Oh, I see! That's interesting.
vocab: いいね | That's nice / Sounds good
example: いいね！いっしょに いこう。 | Sounds good! Let's go together.
vocab: ほんとう | really / truth
example: ほんとう？うれしい！ | Really? I'm happy!

[quiz]
quiz: What does いいね mean? | Goodbye | That's nice | Thank you | B | いいね is a positive reaction meaning “That's nice” or “Sounds good.”
