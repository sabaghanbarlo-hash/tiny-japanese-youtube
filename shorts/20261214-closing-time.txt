title: What Time Do You Close? Ask in Japanese
scene: store
cast: mika, alex

[hook]
Mika: Want to know when a shop closes?
[story]
Alex: すみません、なんじまで ですか。 | Excuse me, until what time are you open?
Mika: ごご しちじまで です。 | Until 7 p.m.
Alex: わかりました。 ありがとう！ | Got it. Thanks!
[ending]
Alex: Say it once, then comment done!
