title: 3 Japanese Phrases That Make You Sound Natural
scene: park

[hook]

Mika: にほんごを もっと はなしたい？ | Nihongo o motto hanashitai? | Want to speak more Japanese?

Alex: じゃあ、この みっつを おぼえてね！ | Jaa, kono mittsu o oboete ne! | Then remember these three expressions!

[story]

Mika: そうなんだ！ | Sou nan da! | Oh, really! / I see!

Alex: いいね！ | Ii ne! | That's nice! / Sounds good!

Mika: ほんとう？ | Hontou? | Really?

Alex: ともだちとの かいわで つかえるよ！ | Tomodachi to no kaiwa de tsukaeru yo! | You can use these in conversations with friends!

[practice]

Mika: アレックスは サッカーが すきなんだ。 | Arekkusu wa sakkaa ga suki nan da. | So, you like soccer, Alex.

Alex: そうなんだ！ミカは？ | Sou nan da! Mika wa? | That's right! What about you, Mika?

Mika: わたしは えを かくのが すき！ | Watashi wa e o kaku no ga suki! | I like drawing!

Alex: いいね！ | Ii ne! | That's nice!

[quiz]

Mika: 「ほんとう？」は えいごで なに？ | “Hontou?” wa eigo de nani? | What does “hontou?” mean in English?

Alex: A、Really? B、Goodbye. C、Thank you. | A, Really? B, Goodbye. C, Thank you. | A, Really? B, Goodbye. C, Thank you.

Mika: こたえは A！ | Kotae wa A! | The answer is A!

[ending]

Alex: どの ことばが すき？コメントで おしえてね！ | Dono kotoba ga suki? Komento de oshiete ne! | Which expression do you like? Tell us in the comments!
