title: “Chotto” Can Mean NO in Japanese (Polite Refusal)
scene: street

[story]
Alex: あした、いっしょに ごはん たべる？ | Want to eat together tomorrow?
Mika: あしたは ちょっと… | Tomorrow is a little...
Alex: ちょっと なに？ | A little what?
Mika: 「ちょっと…」は やわらかい「ノー」だよ。 | “Chotto...” is a soft “no.”
Alex: あ、わかった！ | Oh, I get it!
Mika: また こんど ね！ | Next time!

[vocab]
vocab: ちょっと | a little / (soft no)
example: ちょっと まって！ | Wait a moment!
vocab: こんど | next time
example: また こんど！ | Next time!

[quiz]
quiz: What does あしたは ちょっと… usually mean? | Yes, let's go | A soft no | I'm hungry | B | Japanese speakers often refuse softly without saying “no.”
