title: Saying “hai, hai” Can Sound Rude in Japanese!
scene: classroom
cast: mika, alex

[hook]
Alex: Careful! Saying hai twice can sound annoyed.
[story]
Mika: しゅくだい、やった？ | Did you do your homework?
Alex: はい、はい。 | Yes, yes.
Mika: その いいかた、ちょっと おこっている みたい。 | That sounds like you're annoyed.
Alex: ごめん！ はい、やりました！ | Sorry! Yes, I did it!
[ending]
Mika: Say hai once, with a smile! Comment ok!
